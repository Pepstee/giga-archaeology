## Lowest-cost orderly route

### 1. Confirm genuine dormancy

The company must have had no significant accounting transactions. Permitted exceptions include:

- the £1 paid for the subscriber share on incorporation;
- Companies House filing fees;
- late-filing penalties.

Confirm separately whether it ever:

- received sales, grants, interest or any other income;
- bought or sold goods or services;
- paid wages, teachers or contractors;
- paid for advertising;
- held an income-producing asset;
- incurred transactions through a company bank account.

If any of these occurred, stop treating it as dormant and prepare appropriate accounts and tax records.

### 2. Resolve HMRC status immediately — submitted

The official HMRC dormancy notification was submitted on 30 July 2026:

https://www.gov.uk/tell-hmrc-your-company-is-dormant-for-corporation-tax

The HMRC reminder identifies a short accounting period ending 31 October 2025. Check the company's Business Tax Account for every Notice to Deliver a Company Tax Return, not only this reminder.

If HMRC issued a Notice to Deliver:

- a nil Company Tax Return may still be required for the period on the notice;
- telling HMRC the company is dormant does not automatically cancel an existing notice;
- obtain written confirmation of what returns, if any, remain outstanding before dissolution.

Retain the acknowledgement PDF and confirmation email. The notification does not override a separate Notice to Deliver a Company Tax Return if HMRC has issued one; check subsequent correspondence for any such requirement.

### 3. Apply for voluntary strike-off

Official service:

https://www.gov.uk/strike-off-your-company-from-companies-register/apply-to-strike-off

Current published fee: £18.

Before applying:

- ensure the company has no debts other than any unresolved filing penalty;
- close any company bank account and remove any remaining assets;
- resolve HMRC's Corporation Tax record;
- identify everyone legally entitled to receive a copy of the application.

The majority of directors must approve and sign. If there is one director, that director applies.

Within 7 days after applying, send a copy of the application to all legally required interested parties, including shareholders, creditors and relevant authorities where applicable.

HMRC or another creditor can object until unfinished tax or debt matters are resolved.

### 4. Handle the overdue dormant accounts notice

Companies House states that dormant companies must still file annual accounts. The original deadline has passed, so filing now normally triggers the automatic private-company late-filing penalty:

- no more than one month late: £150;
- more than one month but no more than three months late: £375;
- higher bands apply after that.

The statutory notice demands acceptable accounts by 6 August 2026 and warns of prosecution/disqualification if ignored. Voluntary strike-off is not a mechanism for evading an existing legal obligation or debt.

Contact Companies House promptly and state that:

- the company has never traded;
- the director intends to apply for voluntary strike-off;
- first dormant accounts are already overdue;
- clarification is requested on whether Companies House requires those dormant accounts before dissolution and whether a late-filing penalty has already been imposed.
