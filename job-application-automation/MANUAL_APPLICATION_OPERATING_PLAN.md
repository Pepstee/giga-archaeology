# Manual application operating plan

**Status:** planning complete; no applications submitted by this document  
**Date:** 22 July 2026  
**Purpose:** start applying now without waiting for autonomous JAA certification, while preserving truthful claims, protecting the highest-value vacancies, and turning the manual cohort into evidence for the eventual automated system.

## Verdict

The proposal is directionally correct but partially implemented already.

Giga-user already contains the canonical context, memory, corpus, operator doctrine, and evidence-backed external-proof machinery. Market Aligner already performs candidate sourcing and fit scoring as an independent product. JAA-00 through JAA-03 have durable certification evidence; JAA-04 is still in progress. The installed account-research, resume-cover-letter, and humanizer skills cover the main manual application stages.

The inefficient version would invoke every skill on every vacancy, let Humanizer rewrite whole documents, create a second application database inside giga-user, or wait for the entire JAA programme before applying. The corrected design is a gated manual bridge:

```text
Market Aligner ranking
  -> live vacancy and eligibility check
  -> opportunity-proportional employer research
  -> requirement-to-evidence plan
  -> UK application document drafting
  -> bounded Humanizer edit proposals
  -> truth, coverage, ATS, and consistency revalidation
  -> manual submission
  -> exact artefact and receipt capture
  -> later import into JAA's event ledger
```

Giga-user supplies verified knowledge and public-safe voice guidance. It does not become the job-application control plane. Market Aligner/JAA remains the independent owner of vacancy and application state.

## Optimisation target

The mature target remains qualified interviews per eligible, truthfully supported application, followed by offers and time to offer.

During the training cohort there is a second objective: process learning without burning the best opportunities. The selection rule is therefore lexicographic, not a fake probability formula:

1. The vacancy must be live, eligible, acceptable, and worth taking if offered.
2. Claims must be supportable from approved evidence.
3. Training applications come from ranks 15 to 20 before ranks 1 to 9.
4. The small cohort should cover different role narratives and application surfaces.
5. No top vacancy is delayed past a real deadline merely to satisfy the training order.

Fit scores remain queue-ordering signals, not hiring probabilities. They are not outcome-calibrated.

## Knowledge boundary

### Allowed inputs from giga-user

- Explicit identity, location, work-right, and career facts whose status is current.
- Approved evidence items and exact source pointers.
- Verified project artefacts, test results, and public proof.
- Explicitly confirmed preferences and constraints.
- A small approved set of professional writing samples or a derived public-safe voice profile.

### Not allowed as application facts

- Assistant praise, prompt frequency, or model characterisation.
- Predictions from the digital twin that the operator has not confirmed.
- Raw private conversations or unrelated memories.
- Inferred preferences, unsupported metrics, or claims of production adoption.
- A capability inferred only from the sophistication of an AI-generated system.

Corpus retrieval may discover evidence candidates. It cannot approve them. A candidate becomes application-safe only after its source and wording pass the evidence ledger's standard.

## One-time preparation gate

Resolve only facts that can block real forms or documents. Do not postpone applications to complete the whole profiler.

Required before the first submission:

- current employment status and availability date;
- degree classification and official graduation date;
- exact contact details and approved LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio links;
- exact personal contribution boundaries for each flagship project;
- confirmation that internships, fixed-term, support, and solutions roles in the training cohort are acceptable;
- commute and hybrid limits for any non-remote role.

Salary floor and broader employer preferences can remain unresolved until a vacancy or form asks for them. Unknown answers remain unknown; they are not guessed.

Create one internal evidence inventory, not a generic CV. Each vacancy receives a tailored UK CV compiled from that inventory.

Before the first submission, run the focused LinkedIn preflight in `LINKEDIN_PROFILE_UPDATE_PACK.md`: replace the headline and About section, feature the existing technical proof, add the verified projects, and reorder the lead skills. This is a consistency gate, not a new workstream. Do not delay applications for a new photograph, banner, public case study, or every unresolved historical detail.

## Per-vacancy workflow

### 1. Admission gate

Before research or writing:

- open the official vacancy and verify that it is live;
- record the official URL, capture time, closing date, and a content snapshot or hash;
- verify UK residence compatibility, work rights, location, contract type, and any travel requirement;
- reject duplicates, expired listings, aggregators with no resolvable official route, and roles the operator would not accept;
- list any blocker that requires operator confirmation.

The current scored data has 25 fit explanations that incorrectly describe UK work authorisation as unknown, while profile v1.4 confirms settled status. The application gate must use the canonical profile, not those stale explanations.

### 2. Research gate

Use account-research only after admission passes.

For training vacancies, collect the minimum evidence needed to answer: what the company does, why the role exists, what the team appears to need, and whether any fresh signal changes the opportunity verdict. Keep URLs, dates, contradictions, and confidence.

Use deeper team, decision-maker, product, and public-leverage research only for protected high-value roles or where it will materially improve a requested cover letter or outreach. This avoids spending premium research effort on a role that may fail eligibility or not require a letter.

### 3. Requirement-to-evidence plan

Build a compact matrix before writing:

| Requirement | Essential or preferred | Evidence IDs | Exact safe claim | Gap treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Every essential requirement is marked covered, honestly absent, or blocking. Evidence similarity alone cannot turn project exposure into professional production experience.

The plan also chooses the role narrative. Current likely narratives include AI automation, technical solutions, backend/Python, and cloud/infrastructure. A vacancy gets one coherent narrative, not every possible identity at once.

### 4. Document and answer drafting

Use resume-cover-letter for UK ATS-safe construction from the locked requirement and evidence plan.

- Produce a vacancy-specific CV, normally two pages.
- Write a cover letter only when requested or when company-specific evidence gives it a clear expected benefit.
- Draft form answers from the same approved claims and standard factual answer bank.
- Preserve exact job keywords when they are truthfully supported.
- Never invent numbers to satisfy achievement-bullet conventions. Qualitative results are preferable to false precision.

Use separate drafter and reviewer passes. A reviewer can reject or narrow a claim but cannot create evidence.

### 5. Bounded Humanizer pass

Humanizer is not the last authority and does not receive permission to rewrite the whole pack opaquely.

Use it most heavily on cover letters, motivation answers, and short-form responses. Use it sparingly on CV bullets, where factual density and ATS terminology matter more than personality.

Each accepted edit must identify the exact old span, replacement, reason, touched claim IDs, and touched requirement IDs. Reject an edit if it:

- adds or changes a person, company, skill, credential, metric, date, or work-right fact;
- weakens an evidence-backed ATS term;
- changes the scope of personal contribution;
- removes requirement coverage;
- imitates a generic "human" voice instead of the operator's approved professional register.

After edits, rerun truth, dates, metrics, eligibility, requirement coverage, and consistency checks. Humanizer improves voice; it never approves release.

### 6. Final manual release check

The operator reviews one compact release packet:

- vacancy and eligibility verdict;
- requirement coverage and known gaps;
- exact CV, letter if used, and form answers;
- Humanizer edit diff;
- unresolved risks;
- destination and official application route.

Manual submission is then performed in the browser. No Playwright or browser-use submission is part of this bridge.

### 7. Receipt and learning capture

Retain the exact submitted files and answers, not the latest generic versions. Capture:

- vacancy snapshot and official URL;
- final artefact hashes;
- submitted form answers;
- submission time and official confirmation or screenshot;
- application identifier if issued;
- time spent by stage;
- blockers, corrections, and newly encountered form questions;
- later status, feedback, interview, rejection, or offer evidence.

These are import-ready external events for JAA. Do not create a second mutable career-state database in giga-user and do not write into the active factory checkout while it is certifying.

## Training sequence and promotion gates

Use a sequential cohort, not a simultaneous batch. The current first sequence is:

1. Rank 19, Freelancerprox, Python Developer Intern. Proceed only if the official route and employer legitimacy resolve cleanly.
2. Rank 16, Zscaler, Associate Sales Engineer. This tests the technical-to-commercial narrative and structured motivation answers.
3. Rank 15, Graphcore, Graduate IT Infrastructure Engineer. This tests the cloud/infrastructure narrative and a Greenhouse-style application.

If a vacancy fails the live, legitimacy, eligibility, or genuine-interest gate, replace it with another acceptable role from ranks 15 to 20. A failed gate is useful pipeline evidence, not a reason to force an application.

Promotion is based on process correctness, not employer response, so it does not create a multi-week delay:

- Move to the next training application after the previous pack has an exact receipt and its post-application audit is complete.
- Move into ranks 10 to 14 after two consecutive clean submissions.
- Move into ranks 1 to 9 after three clean submissions covering at least two distinct application surfaces.
- Override the ordering when a protected vacancy has a real imminent deadline, provided the minimum truth and eligibility gates pass.

A clean submission means: live and eligible vacancy, no unsupported claim, no Humanizer fact drift, essential requirements explicitly handled, ATS-critical truthful terms retained, consistent form answers, and an official receipt captured.

## Feedback loop

After every application, update the process assets, not the candidate facts by implication:

- add newly encountered factual questions to the answer bank;
- add verified evidence only through the evidence-approval route;
- record missing evidence and recurring requirement gaps;
- record document or form friction for future JAA fixtures;
- retain outcomes for later fit and strategy calibration;
- do not change scoring policy from a single rejection or silence.

Review after applications 1, 3, 5, and 10. The early reviews optimise correctness and operator time. Later reviews compare response, screen, and interview conversion by role narrative and application strategy.
