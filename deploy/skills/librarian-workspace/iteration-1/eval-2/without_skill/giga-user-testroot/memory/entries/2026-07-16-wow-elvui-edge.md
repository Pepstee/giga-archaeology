AI in 2026 ≈ WoW in 2009: tooling (the "addons") is about to commoditise — the ElvUI moment.
When it does, the differentiator moves to what the elite ran underneath: logs and review
discipline (warcraftlogs, not addons, made the 0.1%). Therefore the durable personal edge is:
owned corpus, calibration ledger, verification instinct, retrospectives over one's own
reasoning — not tool collection. This idea is the founding rationale of the whole GIGA
programme [[2026-07-16-standing-decisions-orchestrator]].
