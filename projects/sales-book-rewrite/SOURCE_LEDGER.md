# Source ledger

## Finding

The old manuscript has the source hierarchy backwards. A compact, distinctive notebook was
fed into ChatGPT, then expanded into a long generic sales manual. The model added whole
domains that were weakly connected or unconnected to the notes, including generic CRM use,
upselling, cross-selling, retention, social-media prospecting and broad "win-win" negotiation
advice. It also converted blunt field language into corporate filler.

The rewrite must therefore return to the notes and treat the old chapters as a contamination
check, not a draft to polish.

## Tier B: authorial instructions

The following short prompts do not add sales knowledge, but they define the intended book:

- The book should be based on this knowledge.
- It should be specific, detailed and straight to the point.
- It should explain why a technique works and what purpose it serves.
- Diagrams and comparison tables are welcome when they clarify a mechanism.
- It should include the five steps, eight steps, FUGI and SCOTSMAN if source material exists.
- The conclusion should warn readers about MLMs and pyramid schemes.

These are binding editorial requirements. They do not authorise the model to invent missing
definitions.

## Tier C: secondary material

### Legacy AI draft

ChatGPT generated a rapid chapter sequence on 2 November, largely in response to one-word
prompts such as `Next`. On 4 November it produced another long sequence after an uploaded
DOCX was summarised. Those assistant turns are preserved in
`sources/legacy-ai-draft-turns.jsonl`.

Permitted use: check whether a subject was expected somewhere in the old book.

Forbidden use: treating its language, examples, explanations or unsupported subject matter as
Artiom's knowledge.

### Pasted web articles

Five very long human-side turns on 2 November are copied Fullinfo articles about consultative
selling, objections, qualification, closes and Feel Felt Found. Their presence on the human
side of the transcript does not make them original notes. They are external source material
and require normal citation and rights-safe paraphrase if used at all.

### Ilya Yablonsky sales-training transcript

On 23 July 2026 the operator supplied a 5,380-word transcript identified as training material
from Ilya Yablonsky. It records a setter/qualification call for a $15,000–$20,000
property-development mentorship. The transcript is analysed in
`analysis/ilya-yablonsky-setter-call-analysis.md`.

Permitted use: an attributed external case study of discovery, financial qualification,
decision-authority checks, identity commitment, price preframing, setter-to-closer handoff and
ethical risk.

Forbidden use: presenting its techniques as knowledge recorded during the author's five months
at Saleshub/Appco, copying its wording, or treating unverified company performance claims as fact.

### Uploaded DOCX

The export records an upload named `Art of Sales. My knowledge..docx`, MIME type DOCX, size
88,932 bytes. The file asset itself is absent from the export and was not found elsewhere on
the machine. Only the upload metadata and ChatGPT's brief description survived.

This is a real gap. The rewrite can proceed because the notebook and generated chapter history
survive, but the missing file cannot be quoted, compared or treated as recovered.

## Known contamination and exclusions

- The old chapters on CRM systems, social-media lead generation, generic retention,
  cross-selling and upselling are excluded unless a primary note or a new attributed source
  justifies them.
- ChatGPT supplied an incorrect replacement list when asked to recall the eight direct-sales
  steps. The notebook's list is authoritative: positive attitude, time management, preparation,
  100 percent effort, know the site, take action, have a target, take control.
- FUGI and SCOTSMAN are named by Artiom in the book-building prompts, but their full
  definitions do not appear in the recovered primary notes. They remain source gaps rather
  than invitations to copy ChatGPT's definitions.
- The 7/38/55 communication ratio is recorded as training doctrine. It must not be presented as
  a universal scientific law without checking the original research and its narrow context.
- Charity statistics and funding claims belong to October 2024 pitch drafts. Verify them
  against dated charity sources before publication.
- Manipulative techniques are historical evidence. The book should analyse them openly, not
  disguise them as neutral best practice.
