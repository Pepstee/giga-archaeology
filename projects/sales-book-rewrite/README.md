# Art of Sales rewrite

This project reconstructs the sales book from Artiom's own field notes rather than polishing
the ChatGPT-written manuscript produced in November 2024.

Source order:

1. Timestamped operator-authored notebook turns and pitches.
2. Operator-authored instructions about scope, tone and omissions.
3. The legacy AI manuscript only as a coverage and contamination check.
4. External material only when it is separately sourced, verified and clearly attributed.

The legacy title was `Art of Sales. My knowledge.` The working title is:

> **Art of Sales: Notes from Five Months in Direct Sales**

## Files

- `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`: provenance, exclusions and unresolved gaps.
- `VOICE_SPEC.md`: voice and humanizer constraints.
- `ARCHITECTURE.md`: evidence-grounded replacement structure.
- `sources/primary-turns.jsonl`: the eleven selected primary-source turns.
- `sources/legacy-ai-draft-turns.jsonl`: the November AI prose, retained for audit only.
- `sources/upload-metadata-conversation.json`: evidence for the missing uploaded DOCX.

No sentence from the legacy AI draft is authoritative merely because it appeared in the old
book. Claims must trace back to the primary notes or to a separately verified source.
