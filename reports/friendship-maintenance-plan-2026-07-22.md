# Friendship maintenance plan

Date: 22 July 2026  
Basis: native Telegram, Discord, Instagram, both ChatGPT exports, Claude export, and the
post-Telegram friendship ranking.

## Governing principle

Closeness grows through repeated reality: private initiative, shared experience, remembered
follow-up, mutual disclosure and repair. The principal failure mode is not simple silence. It is
turning friends into projects, functions, rankings, audiences or subjects of analysis. Do not show
friends private corpus-derived rankings or quote forgotten messages back at them without consent.

## Andrei / Bottle — preserve equality inside the strongest bond

The bond already has exceptional frequency and breadth. More messages are not the requirement.
Its risk is the Pygmalion pattern: interpreting, steering or trying to awaken/improve Andrei until
conversation becomes instruction.

- Ask about the issue currently occupying him and return to it later without offering a system
  unless he asks.
- Keep one shared future concrete—trip, visit or small joint undertaking—with equal ownership.
- Occasionally disclose an unresolved feeling before converting it into a theory or plan.
- Protect ordinary nonsense and banter; it is part of the intimacy, not wasted bandwidth.

Immediate move: arrange one voice call centred primarily on his present life, then follow up on
one detail a week later.

## Serge / Serjo — choose him privately, not only through the group

Serge supplies embodied ease, invitations, affection and social momentum. Historically, he has
reopened substantially more direct sessions. The risk is that a real brotherhood becomes mostly
group-mediated and banter-protected.

- Initiate one-to-one contact without waiting for Brotein Shake to generate an occasion.
- Invite him to a walk, drink, meal or activity specifically as a pair.
- After the jokes, ask one sincere second question about work, family, relationships or direction.
- Say affection plainly when it is true; this relationship already tolerates direct brother-language.

Immediate move: invite him to one simple one-to-one outing and choose the date yourself.

## Allen — add present-tense intimacy to durable specialist trust

Allen carries shared mastery history, technical language and difficult relationship knowledge.
The bond survives through games, media, analysis and specialist exchange. Its risk is remaining
attached to old systems or third-party drama rather than each person's current interior life.

- Alternate links, memes and technical talk with a direct personal check-in.
- Ask for his judgment on something real, act on it where appropriate, and later tell him what
  happened. This makes trust consequential.
- A joint technical build or game session would fit the bond, but leave time afterward for life.
- Name his reliability once, directly and without grandiosity.

Immediate move: propose a call or build/game session, then reserve the final part for what each of
you is actually trying to do this year.

## Lino / Dima — convert the imagined reunion into reality

The relationship has decade-scale history, strong bilateral disclosure and mutual future talk,
but remains remote and partly mediated by forwarded politics. Its greatest opportunity is to cross
from imagined meeting to embodied memory.

- Replace some forwarded news with original updates, voice calls and questions about his actual day.
- Turn the Elbrus/reunion idea into a feasibility conversation: timing, leave, documents, money and
  a safe realistic location. Do not let it remain permanent mythology.
- Check in about health, military/work conditions and relationships without immediately debating
  the worldview surrounding them.
- Create a smaller fallback meeting if the ambitious trip is not feasible.

Immediate move: schedule a proper call whose purpose is to choose either a realistic meeting window
or the next concrete prerequisite.

## Joel — build friendship between equals

Joel is the chosen, future-facing friend: travel, projects, group action and explicit commitment.
The danger is hierarchical care—elevating him strategically, comparing status, or treating him as
someone whose trajectory should be organised.

- Let him lead an outing or project and follow his plan without silently scoring it.
- Spend time one-to-one, outside the audience and hierarchy of the group.
- Follow through on one adventure already discussed rather than generating another abstract plan.
- Ask about his relationship and goals as his friend, not as a strategist managing his position.
- Offer direct appreciation that is about who he is, not his usefulness or potential.

Immediate move: ask him to choose the next shared activity and commit to the date he selects.

## Oleg / Bebop — make him more than an emergency vault

Oleg held the deepest retained crisis disclosure, but everyday integration is thin. Reconnection
should be reciprocal and low-pressure, not another sudden transfer of distress.

- Send a concise update acknowledging that his presence mattered during the earlier period.
- Ask what changed in his life and give his answer as much space as your own update.
- Create occasional ordinary contact—game, call, congratulations, shared reference—so the channel
  is not activated only by catastrophe.
- Do not attempt to recreate the exact intensity of 2022; allow the present relationship to define
  itself.

Immediate move: send a short message of gratitude plus a genuine question about his current life.

## Definition / Anton — choose repair or accept history

This was a highly entangled shadow-twin friendship whose intimacy travelled through irony,
philosophical combat and mutual character-making. The later form became more adversarial and
group-bound. Greater closeness is possible only if both people want less theatre and more directness.

- Decide first whether renewed individual closeness is genuinely desired, rather than demanded by
  historical importance.
- If yes, initiate privately and name one thing you valued without psychoanalysing him.
- Acknowledge one concrete rupture or antagonistic pattern if it still matters.
- Build a new shared activity rather than trying to resurrect the old dyad wholesale.
- Preserve boundaries if rivalry, contempt or surveillance returns.

Immediate move: either send one sincere private invitation, or consciously classify the bond as an
important historical friendship and stop treating non-revival as failure.

## Sustainable maintenance floor

- Each week, initiate one meaningful private exchange with a friend who has recently carried more
  of the reopening burden. Rotate rather than messaging everyone performatively.
- Each month, create one one-to-one call or embodied activity that is not merely a group spillover.
- When a friend discloses something important, follow up later without waiting for them to repeat it.
- Each quarter, complete one shared real-world event: visit, trip, build, game session, meal or
  difficult conversation.
- Keep the system private and lightweight. It should help memory; it must not turn care into a KPI.

Priority order for the next month: Serge one-to-one; Joel-led activity; Lino reunion call; Allen
personal-plus-craft call. Maintain Andrei organically. Reopen Oleg and Definition only with genuine
interest, not out of obligation to the ranking.
