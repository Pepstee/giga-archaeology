The major correction is immediate: Discord makes 2022–23 the densest observed social period
in the whole personal corpus. It also provides 5,058 messages for 2024. Any account that
treated those years as a thin prelude was an artefact of source availability, not the data.

## What this export does and does not prove

Discord states that the package contains messages sent by the account. It does not include
the other side of conversations, and deleted messages are absent. The timestamps contain no
timezone marker.

Most importantly, **sent is not the same as composed**. The corpus visibly contains:

- copypastas posted repeatedly across channels, sometimes with names or details altered;
- quotations and forwarded incoming messages, including messages addressed to “Artiom”;
- text written in a feminine first person and then reposted for discussion;
- English-learning prompts, lesson materials and transcripts;
- AI-formatted analyses and instructions;
- commands, code, links and documents.

The raw view therefore labels every record `operator_account_outgoing` and
`composition_status: unverified`. No passage enters a behavioural fine-tune merely because
the account sent it. Pasted code and documents still count as evidence of what the operator
was doing, preserving or attending to; they do not prove sole authorship or endorsement.

## Five strong first-pass findings

### 1. Discord was a lived social world in 2022–23, not a peripheral chat tool

The account sent 73,489 messages in those two years. DMs form roughly 72–74% of the traffic,
but public guild channels were also substantial. The content is dominated by World of
Warcraft raids, guild governance, status, loyalty, tactics, server politics, friendship,
copypasta and performance.

This is evidence of extensive social participation, coalition management and public persona
experimentation. It is not evidence that every extreme or theatrical statement was believed.
Repeated modified pastes show that provocation and stylised performance were activities in
their own right.

There was also real administrative and moral reasoning inside the gaming frame. On 5 Sep
2022 the account sent a long causal history of a guild decision and the obligations created by
recruiting others (`message 1016468079645048852`). On 9 Dec it sent a careful apology that
distinguished intention, foreseeable harm, evidence and future conduct
(`message 1050873450228629515`). The later “systems” style was already present, but applied to
a game community with real social stakes.

### 2. Vulnerability is repeatedly converted into analysis, argument and proof

The clearest early instance is the July 2023 relationship sequence. The account enumerated
double standards, distrust, gaslighting, responsibility and distorted perception, then built
a numbered counter-case asking whether it might itself be wrong (`messages
1129168524317839360` and `1129169772483977286`). The counterfactual included an absolute
self-condemnation: if wrong, it would mean being stupid, selfish and not worth another
person's time.

That combination matters more than the amateur diagnosis used in the dispute. Emotional
threat is handled by constructing a case, defining terms, listing observations, anticipating
objections and trying to close every escape route. Analysis functions both as genuine
self-audit and as armour against uncertainty.

The same mechanics recur in March 2026. A contraception dispute was broken into numbered
claims, competing interpretations and a long AI-styled assessment, which was then sent into
the relationship (`messages 1485287972667523144` through `1485288368446374048`). The content
may be AI-assisted or pasted; the behaviour—seeking an external analytic adjudicator and
deploying its result inside the conflict—is directly evidenced.

### 3. The beliefs about women and relationships were visible well before December 2025

Discord materially extends the genealogy of the later beliefs. A 25 Nov 2023 DM framed a
woman's wish for marriage and a child as egocentric and interpreted her time-pressure as
failure to recognise the man's feelings (`message 1177960737449201684`). Other sent texts in
2023 contain much broader hostile claims about women; some are quotations or copied
philosophical passages and must not automatically be attributed as original belief.

The strongest likely personal evidence is private and self-implicating. On 17 Jul 2024 the
account described a relationship as materially and sexually satisfactory, then explicitly
said the remaining problem was its own: it had not “had enough experience” outside the
relationship (`message 1263119926814576754`). On 29 Jul it questioned the point of a serious
relationship at 21 and said marriage was not planned (`message 1267565638369411174`).

Thus the late-2025 worldview was not created in one bad December. Discord suggests a longer
line: relationship conflict, gender generalisation, perceived asymmetry of obligation,
ambivalence about commitment, and the use of comparison or optionality to protect autonomy.

### 4. 2024 is a pivot, not an empty year

Message volume falls from 35,175 in 2023 to 5,058 in 2024—an 85.6% decline. That is a change
in where life was being lived and recorded, not absence of development.

The surviving record mixes four worlds:

1. continuing gaming friendships and guild culture;
2. a relationship that is stable in practical terms but internally ambivalent;
3. university/course material and technical coordination;
4. English teaching/learning and paid-work scripts.

On 6 Oct 2024 the account sent a detailed plan for an English lesson: repeated reading,
pronunciation correction, comprehension checks and adaptation to the learner's preference
for strict feedback (`message 1292592646526730311`). Whether every sentence was independently
composed is less important than the demonstrated role: coordinating a learning process,
setting standards and turning tacit practice into an explicit method.

The long 18 Jan 2024 reflection on another guild leader's archive is also notable (`message
1197471267830378556`). It describes recording and carefully sorting stories not merely as
memory preservation but as the ultimate purpose of a public role. Even if the subject is
another person, it shows that archive-making, performance and retrospective meaning were
already salient concepts.

### 5. Later Discord becomes narrower, more instrumental and more formal

The raw mean message length rises from 26–27 characters in 2022–23 to 40 in 2024, 140 in 2025
and 104 in 2026. This is **not** clean evidence of intellectual maturation: 2025–26 contain
many long English exercises, lesson materials, forwarded documents and AI output. The valid
finding is a platform-role change. Discord moves away from constant casual sociality toward
selected DMs, education, coordination, work, reference material and occasional intensive
disputes.

Concrete autobiographical fragments include:

- a June 2025 report of being called back for an energy-sales job
  (`message 1384985836264751134`);
- an October 2025 report of two days in a Cannock warehouse
  (`message 1423060357013835786`);
- a January 2026 English answer describing a full-time warehouse job as well paid but not a
  desired long-term career (`message 1458192559074574482`);
- a February 2026 note that the academic year would end near April and one day would be
  needed to defend the diploma (`message 1473274285085687951`).

The source therefore shows the transition from game-community competence to education,
employment and career dissatisfaction in unusually concrete increments.

## Continuities and changes

### Continuities

- Systems thinking existed before the current technical programme: guild politics, raid
  planning and relationship conflict were already modelled causally.
- The account repeatedly preserves, reposts and reorganises material. The later knowledge
  infrastructure has a social and archival prehistory.
- Public performance and private seriousness coexist. The same month can contain absurd
  copypasta, careful apologies, tactical analysis and personal vulnerability.
- Status, competence and not being made a fool of are durable sensitivities.
- Under relational threat, the account seeks an objective frame, proof, a decisive verdict
  and protection from unilateral blame.

### Changes

- Social breadth contracts sharply after 2023; fewer relationships and channels carry more
  of the remaining traffic.
- English use expands from occasional communication into sustained educational practice.
- The subjects of systemisation move from raids and guilds toward teaching, university,
  work, career and AI-mediated self-analysis.
- Message use becomes more instrumental: coordination, materials, transcripts and analysed
  disputes replace much of the continuous banter.
- The operator increasingly recruits external systems—documents, structured methods and
  eventually AI—to stabilise judgement.

## Fine-tuning consequence

This is a valuable corpus, but it is not direct reply supervision and it is not yet a clean
style corpus. Before any training use it needs a composition-and-function pass with at least
these labels:

- `likely_original_casual`
- `likely_original_reflective`
- `likely_original_work_or_study`
- `quoted_or_forwarded`
- `copypasta_or_performance`
- `ai_generated_or_ai_assisted`
- `code_command_or_reference`
- `attachment_only`
- `ambiguous`

Year, channel type and public/private register must remain attached. Public guild performance
must not define the current casual voice; relationship material must not silently enter a
general adapter; English-learning errors must not be trained as target prose; AI output must
not be relabelled as the operator's natural reasoning.

The audit found 19 possible credential-bearing texts, 2,054 URL flags, 2,143 phone-pattern
flags, 796 handle flags and 14 email flags. These are review queues, not confirmed leaks, but
they make direct upload or training impermissible until resolved.

## Next evidential pass

The next pass should work backwards month by month from July 2026 to August 2022, adding only
Discord-supported events and thoughts to the existing longitudinal ledger. For each month it
should separate:

1. actions and relationships;
2. original-looking statements;
3. pasted or performed material as behavioural signs;
4. emotional state and uncertainty;
5. changes to the prior characterisation;
6. contradictions and source limitations.

That pass—not raw volume or keyword counts—should decide which previous developmental stages
survive and where 2023–24 must be rewritten.
