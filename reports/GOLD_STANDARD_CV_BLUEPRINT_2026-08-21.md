### Reuse policy

1. Reuse the exact PDF for a general application when vacancy-specific tailoring would not materially improve the truthful match.
2. For a tailored CV, reuse its layout, hierarchy, typography, tone and still-current wording as the starting point. Exact paragraphs and bullets may be reused verbatim; novelty is not a requirement.
3. Tailoring may reorder, compress or replace content only when the vacancy and evidence justify it. Compare every derivative against this PDF for visual and narrative regression.
4. This PDF is the presentation baseline, not a new claims authority. The candidate evidence ledger, operator confirmations and current repository evidence still govern whether a claim may be used. If a claim becomes stale, correct the derivative rather than preserving it for visual consistency.
5. Never silently overwrite this artifact. A successor becomes the gold standard only after explicit operator approval and must record its new hash here.

## Layout specification

- A4, two pages, single column.
- 18 to 20 mm side margins and 15 to 17 mm top and bottom margins.
- Candidate name at 20 to 22 pt, identity line at 10.5 to 11 pt, body at 9.5 to 10 pt with at least 12 pt leading.
- Use Aptos, Arial, Calibri, Helvetica or another standard sans-serif typeface.
- Use one restrained accent colour and dark body text. The current teal treatment is appropriate.
- Use standard headings: `Profile`, `Engineering focus`, `Selected engineering work`, `Education and research`, `Selected additional engineering`, `Languages`.
- No repeated page-two name, `CV` label, header or footer.
- No icons, columns, tables, skill bars, star ratings, photographs or decorative sidebars in the production CV.
- Keep bullets to two lines where possible and never split a project entry across pages.
- Keep project-specific links only when the linked repository or case study is public, scrubbed, recruiter-readable and consistent with the claim.
