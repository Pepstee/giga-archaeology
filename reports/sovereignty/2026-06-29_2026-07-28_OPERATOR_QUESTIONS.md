# Sovereignty baseline operator questions

Reply with the compact answer format at the bottom. Percentages may be rough; use `?` when you
cannot estimate. The assessment will preserve uncertainty rather than force precision.

## A. Ratify the major ledger

From C01-C20 in the commitment ledger:

1. Which IDs were **major commitments** during 29 Jun-28 Jul?
2. Which of those were **not genuinely chosen or later endorsed by you**?
3. Which should be treated as subprojects rather than separate commitments?
4. For C06 and C15-C20, state the actual outcome by 28 Jul in one phrase each.

## B. Choice authenticity

For the ratified major commitments as a group:

5. In how many did you have at least one **concrete executable alternative**?
6. How many could you have refused or renegotiated **without unacceptable material, legal,
   relationship, or safety damage**?
7. If money, approval/status pressure, sunk cost, and immediate environmental pressure vanished,
   how many would you still choose?
8. Of commitments already present on 29 Jun, how many did you still endorse after at least
   30 days?
9. How many had material contrary behaviour: repeated avoidance, escape planning, recurring
   resentment, or actions inconsistent with the declared choice?
10. Rate your practical power to refuse unwanted demands during the period, 0-100.
11. Rate how recognisably yours the life you lived felt, 0-100.

## C. Time, options, and capacity not present locally

12. Roughly what share of your discretionary time was consciously chosen, 0-100%?
13. Roughly what share of tracked waking attention went to deliberately selected activity,
    0-100%, and what share went to compulsive feeds, 0-100%?
14. On how many of the 30 days did you have at least 30 minutes of protected, unstructured time?
15. Financial runway in months at period end.
16. Number of genuinely executable alternatives to your current work/living path, capped at 3.
17. Largest share of any critical area controlled by one provider, client, gatekeeper, or route,
    0-100%.
18. Geographic/legal mobility, 0-100: practical ability to live, work, travel, and maintain
    documents across the places you intended.
19. Portable-income share, 0-100%.
20. Critical tools that were substitutable / total critical tools.
21. Major decisions that were reversible / total major decisions.
22. Median days needed to regain your intended plan after a meaningful disruption, and the
    number of slack-buffer days you had at period end.

## D. Life floors

23. Create commitments met / due; Relate commitments met / due; Inhabit commitments met / due.
24. Completed work packets with a real user, client, employer, learner, publication, or
    independent consequence / all completed work packets.
25. Rate the period 0-100 for life satisfaction, life feeling worthwhile, and affect balance
    (positive minus negative overall, translated to 0-100).
26. On how many of the 30 days did you meet your own minimum for sleep, movement, nutrition, and
    recovery together?
