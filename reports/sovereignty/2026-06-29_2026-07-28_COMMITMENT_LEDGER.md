## Evidence rules

- `receipt` means an inspectable action or outcome exists.
- `statement` means the operator explicitly expressed the commitment.
- `standing` means the commitment is present in the canonical context.
- `derived` is included only to locate a possible commitment and cannot ratify it.
- Work generated by an AI under operator direction is described as directed/accepted work,
  not hand-written work.
- Original exports are read-only. Exact-message references below point into the derived,
  source-addressed unified-memory database.

## Safe findings before operator answers

1. **Creation has much stronger receipt density than administration, capacity, or attention.**
   GIGA, JAA/orchestrator, the unified corpus, LoRA, job-application materials, the sales-book
   reconstruction, and the English-teaching analysis all left inspectable artefacts.
2. **External reality contact exists but is narrower than internal production.** Confirmed
   consequences include a submitted job application, real lessons, travel bookings, dental
   treatment, a genuine-but-abandoned client project, and a tool shipped for Hyun.
3. **The open administrative cluster is real and explicit.** The UK flight, driving test,
   Russian passport appointment, and Romanian renewal were captured as commitments, but local
   evidence does not establish completion.
4. **The largest measurement blind spot is time.** No trustworthy calendar/time-use/screen-time
   ledger covers the window, so attention, discretionary-time authorship, value allocation, and
   capacity cannot be inferred from message volume.
5. **Self-deception cannot be scored from declared goals alone.** The ledger supplies behavioural
   receipts and contradictions, but refusal cost, counterfactual endorsement, and delayed
   re-endorsement require direct operator answers.

## Baseline admission decision

The model must withhold the headline Sovereignty Index at this stage. Filling missing values with
zero would convert “not visible locally” into “did not happen”; filling them from retrospective
interpretation would turn hypotheses into autobiography. The attached question packet requests
only values that cannot be derived safely from the current local evidence.
