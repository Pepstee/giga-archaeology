Decision: completely stop non-purposeful feed scrolling. Do not silently weaken this into a
generic "use social media less" objective or a time-boxed scrolling allowance.

Rationale: the operator explicitly identified complete cessation as a need, consistent with
the 21 Jul efficiency audit's finding that scrolling consumes time while producing neither
output nor reliable restoration. Rejected alternative: merely budgeting a smaller scrolling
window. The exact technical enforcement boundary still needs to be operationalised so that
purposeful messaging, search and publishing are not accidentally blocked.
