The memory bot's end-state, ratified in his words: organic growth into a digitalised self.
Five stages, in order:
1. **Memories** — corpus-derived entries (running now, [[2026-07-18-memory-derived-not-dictated]]).
2. **Accuracy** — eliminate false memories and mistakes (his flight-confusion worry is the
   canonical case: cite sources, name gaps, never bridge by plausibility).
3. **Speaking style** — adopt his registers (casual trainable now,
   [[2026-07-18-virtual-artiom-modes]]).
4. **Thinking style** — adopt his reasoning patterns (Session 04's retrospective machinery
   is the training ground).
5. **Self-scrutiny** — put his own reasoning, thinking, intelligence under analysis; find
   the gaps in his own brain. Then, mature enough, it lives on his computer and works
   instead of him.
Requirement attached: context and memory must be MODEL-AGNOSTIC — plain files, portable
across Claude/GPT/Kimi/local; the Telegram bot carries all memory and all context, not a
subset. No platform lock-in at any layer.
Source: operator, verbatim, 18 Jul 2026.
