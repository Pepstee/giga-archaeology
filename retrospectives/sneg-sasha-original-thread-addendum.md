## The relationship's shape

It begins as game-specific contact, becomes exceptionally dense in February and March 2022,
and then broadens into daily companionship:

- arena and raid planning;
- apologies after performance conflict;
- food, sleep and smoking;
- music, films and series watched together;
- late-night calls and mutual availability;
- teasing, affectionate address and missing her;
- uncertainty about whether they would spend time together.

The bond was therefore not a fantasy inferred from a birthday message. It had repeated,
mundane co-presence—the strongest evidence that an online relationship had become part of
daily life.

## What the outgoing thread establishes

High confidence:

1. Artiom and Sasha had a meaningful, high-frequency bond by February–May 2022.
2. Performance conflict occurred, and he sometimes behaved badly during losses.
3. He attempted repair, took substantial blame and proposed changing activities to prevent
   recurrence.
4. By early May he wanted explicit coordination and found ambiguity aversive.
5. Sasha became less responsive in June; he worried something had happened.
6. He experienced the rupture as a major loss and spent half a month composing her birthday
   message.
7. On 3 August he believed he had betrayed her trust and said he could no longer reciprocate.

Not established:

- what Sasha said in the missing incoming messages;
- whether either person explicitly promised exclusivity;
- what Artiom did that he believed betrayed her trust;
- whether her silence was unilateral ghosting, a response to that event, or both at different
  points;
- what exactly she asked for on 3 August.

## The birthday message

The complete message is record
`message:discord:teen_vstee:850117460459257896:996579589105139752`.

Its story is restrained grief. Artiom wishes her a happy future, tells her that other people
will be available to help her, says he still remembers their shared time pleasantly, reveals
that he rehearsed the moment for half a month, calls her the greatest loss of his life and
ends by affirming that she is a wonderful girl and good person. There is no joke or revenge
in it.

## The August reversal

The 3 August sequence prevents a morally simple reconstruction. At first he says he withdrew
because he inferred that he disgusted her, calls himself valueless and describes silent
suffering. Then he says he is ashamed and cannot apologise for betraying her trust. Finally
he refuses reciprocity, says the feeling is gone and that only guilt remains.

This can be read as three rapid positions:

1. **Pre-emptive self-rejection:** “you lost nothing valuable.”
2. **Moral self-condemnation:** “I betrayed your trust.”
3. **Boundary/control restoration:** “I cannot reciprocate anymore.”

The missing incoming turns may make that sequence coherent, but they cannot be reconstructed
from his side alone.

## Developmental significance

Sneg remains a foundational relationship injury, but the recovered evidence makes it less
one-dimensional. Artiom was not only the person deceived and abandoned; within the original
ending he also saw himself as someone who had harmed her, could not be trusted not to repeat
it and no longer wished to resume the bond.

The later Allen disclosure may have replaced this morally ambiguous ending with a cleaner
story of her deception. That cleaner story relieved uncertainty and redistributed blame. It
also made it easier to generalise the event into a doctrine about women. The recovered thread
does not disprove Allen's allegations; it shows why the full psychological meaning cannot be
reduced to them.
