## Lowest and happiest candidates

### Strongest affective low: 2–3 December 2025

`claude_conversations.jsonl:1216-1240` moves from an objectively social day to emptiness,
then to material comparison, “There is nothing about me that I like,” “I think it's just over
for me,” grief about wasted youth, and fear of becoming both burnt out and alienated. The
globality of the condemnation across money, career, appearance, citizenship, home and time
makes this the strongest retained candidate.

September 2025 (`:2186-2212`) may have contained deeper anhedonia: “I just want to not.” But
December shows the sharper collapse of the whole self. On 26–28 December (`:62-136`) the
ideas become morally and philosophically darker—mechanistic action, dehumanisation and a
crisis of meaning—yet the operator simultaneously says “Life's good, but not good enough.”
It is therefore best labelled the intellectual/moral nadir, not necessarily the affective low.

The May–June 2024 unemployment/upskilling sequence (`chatgpt_conversations.jsonl:2556-2558`,
`:32586`, `:33918-33920`) and the £50 starting point in November (`:4464`) establish real
material pressure, but the operator remains future-directed. The de Sade birthday prompt
(`:3813-3815`) is stylised social writing and is rejected as reliable mood evidence.

### Strongest happiness candidate: 24–25 April 2026

`instagram_20260717.jsonl:8666-8683` records Artiom reporting that autonomous agents fixed and
independently tested SCAFAD, followed by “I am so happy you can't even imagine.” The next day
(`:8798-8820`) combines a nap, food, sexual intimacy, playful contact with Hyun, “Working is
fun,” and “Life is good x2.” This is the clearest synchronisation of four recurring needs:
agency, mastery, intimacy and immediate embodied ease.

The broader happiest stage is spring 2026: explicit happiness with Hyun recurs in March–May,
and practical shared-future language becomes common. The peaceful Wolverhampton period in
August 2025 is another high-quality state—autonomous focus, an ordered room, exercise and
“great joy”—but the operator explicitly described it as containing “no deep joy.”

June–October 2024 is the strongest early high candidate: `chatgpt_conversations.jsonl:2660`
calls joining Saleshub a “stellar event”; `:3317`, `:3731` show increasing public leadership;
and `:4120` announces Excellence Exalted and its first teacher. This supports activation,
belonging and momentum, but much of the wording was composed for public presentation and is
therefore weaker evidence of private affect than the April 2026 Instagram record.

## Disconfirming evidence that constrains the profile

- He is not simply vain: he distinguishes personal aesthetic satisfaction from superiority
  and often buys second-hand or rejects clutter.
- He is not simply antisocial: he has sales/tutoring competence, approaches strangers, keeps
  a multi-thread network and becomes intensely communicative with chosen people.
- He is not uniformly unemotional: the Hyun register is affectionate, apologetic, playful and
  practically caring.
- He is not merely a planner who never finishes: SCAFAD layers, tested orchestrator slices,
  Market Aligner, client documents and the GIGA corpus are durable outputs.
- He is not reliably disciplined across everything: his own reports repeatedly distinguish
  obsession-driven bursts from routine follow-through.
- He does not enact every late-December proposition uniformly: loyalty, legality, evidence
  standards and repair coexist with his stated continued agreement with most of the theory.
  Conduct therefore complicates the ideology but does not establish its repudiation.
- The corpus cannot establish diagnosis, IQ, attachment style, present mental health, income,
  relationship quality, or the private experience of happiness outside recorded moments.
