# Teen Discord account — developmental reconstruction, 2018–2022

**Account:** `vstee`, global display name `B L Λ D E`  
**Prepared:** 26 July 2026  
**Evidence:** 248,752 account-owner messages in 485 populated channels  
**Boundary:** one-sided outgoing record; pasted/performed material is not treated as literal
autobiography without contextual support

## What this export changes

The old account changes the biography in three major ways.

First, the “immersive WoW prehistory” is no longer a blank inferred from later recollection.
It is a 248,752-message social world. The centre is not 2022 but 2020–2021: 77,975 text
messages in 2020 and 125,299 in 2021. The operator was not merely playing a game alone. He
was embedded in guilds, high-volume DMs, raid organisation, cross-community status and a
distinct comic language.

Second, social selectivity did not begin as withdrawal. On 14 March 2021 he explicitly said
he had “grown” on the idea that playing everywhere was cool because he knew everyone, stayed
informed and had doors open—comparing it to school popularity. Later concentration into a few
bonds therefore developed out of network centrality, not out of lifelong social incapacity.

Third, the Sneg/Sasha relationship and the acute low of May 2022 are directly present. The
previous report incorrectly treated the relationship as outside the Discord boundary. The
outgoing thread contains 3,476 messages from June 2021 to August 2022, including the dense
relationship period, repair attempts, ordinary co-presence, the full birthday message and the
August ending.

The median teen-era message is extremely short. Meaning is carried by bursts: correction,
question, joke, answer and qualification are often separate packets. Long messages usually
mean one of four different things and must not be collapsed: a serious explanation, a public
performance, creative/roleplay writing, or pasted material.

## Stage 1 — entry and apprenticeship, 2018–2019

The retained 2018 material is too sparse to support a psychological portrait. In 2019 the
account becomes regular: Altek, Eternal Souls raids, Lino, Gefalen and several unknown DMs.
Monthly volume grows from tens in spring to 1,395 in December.

The important developmental fact is not a particular ideology. It is entry into an organised
world with roles, competence tests and recurrent people. A November exchange identifies him
as “Артём, 17, учусь” while discussing data as research rather than a favour. In December he
attempted to leave WoW and gave away his account, apologising that he could no longer be
useful. That early exit attempt already shows the later cycle: intense incorporation into a
domain, loss of desire, decisive attempted severance, then return.

What he was learning:

- belonging through usefulness and specialised knowledge;
- public role and private alliance as two different social layers;
- the pleasure of turning mechanics into explanations;
- the first version of quitting when the world loses charge.

## Stage 2 — immersive mastery and intimate network formation, 2020

2020 is the first complete ecology. There is activity on every day. January is dominated by
Lino; February by the Altek refuge and raids; from April through September Bottle becomes one
of the central private relationships; Gefalen and Fols are also recurrent. By December the
network has widened again, with Serj and 69 active threads.

This is where the durable cognitive template becomes visible. On 4 November he wrote a
1,072-character explanation of rogue hit mechanics: he decomposed direct and indirect
effects, distinguished caps from marginal value and compared competing stat packages. That
is structurally the same move later applied to sales, appearance, relationships and AI:
identify the true mechanic, reject the folk shortcut, trace second-order effects and optimise
the whole system.

But the year is not reducible to mechanics. The account contains:

- thousands of short private packets with Bottle and Gefalen;
- a home-group refuge with its own language and recurring comic symbols;
- guild continuity described through a social metaphor—three elephants standing on a turtle,
  with Bottle as the turtle;
- repeated switching between serious technical authority and deliberate absurdity.

The most important emotional reward appears to have been *inhabiting a consequential shared
world*. Performance mattered because people, memory and rank made it socially real.

## Stage 3 — community centrality and the performed operator, 2021

2021 is the densest year: 125,299 text messages, all 365 days, 250 threads. June alone has
17,936 text messages; November has 13,638. One day, 7 November, contains 1,413 operator
messages, principally an 834-message exchange with Fols.

His own description is decisive:

> “Лично я вообще ‘вырос’ на том факте, что играть везде — круто, ведь ты знаком со
> всеми, всегда в курсе дел … и для тебя всегда открыты везде двери.”

The comparison to school popularity shows that information access, recognition and movement
between groups were not incidental. They were an identity reward.

At the same time, he was sensitive to group ecology. On 7 May he said he was afraid to invite
people because the guild had a harmony that was easy to destroy. In September he noticed a
friend spending less time in a shared space and asked whether something was wrong. This
qualifies the image of a status-only networker: he was monitoring atmosphere, continuity and
withdrawal.

The year develops five capacities:

1. **Cross-group navigation.** He knows multiple scenes rather than belonging to one sealed
   guild.
2. **Technical authority.** Mechanics, roster composition and performance are everyday
   subjects.
3. **Public comic performance.** Hyperbole, copypasta, emoji floods and mock grandeur create
   group identity.
4. **Private maintenance.** High-volume DMs sustain chosen bonds behind the public layer.
5. **Ecological control.** He becomes concerned with who enters, how the group changes and
   whether the social machine remains coherent.

This is the earliest strong form of the later Sovereign Architect, but its material is a
community rather than software.

## Stage 4 — romantic inward turn, crisis and rupture, 2022

### January

The broader network is still dense: 5,239 text messages and 81 threads. The year has not yet
collapsed into the relationship. Roster construction, raids, Bottle, Plaksa and community
threads remain active.

### February–March: Sneg becomes a centre

Sasha's DM becomes the largest February thread (1,254 messages) and remains near the top in
March (1,120). The early period is grounded in WoW—arena performance, raid composition and
mechanics—but rapidly becomes ordinary co-presence: food, sleep, calls, films, music, teasing
and watching series together.

The February 18 repair sequence is revealing. After behaving badly during arena losses, he
apologised, described himself as in an affective state, said there was no justification,
attributed the intensity partly to raised expectations, admitted the past could not be
changed and proposed preventing recurrence by changing the shared activity. This is an early
example of the pattern that survives later: acute self-blame followed by mechanistic
post-mortem and a procedural fix.

It also shows why a simple “cold manipulator” portrait is false. He tried to restore peace,
said he enjoyed playing with her and lowered his own status while raising hers. The problem
is that self-condemnation was global—“я не отрицаю что я еблан”—which can invite reassurance
without producing the slower emotional repair the other person may need.

On 29 March he sent Lino a long first-person reflection on honour, dignity, discipline and
self-respect as intrinsic qualities. The message then developed into fictional/roleplay
material, so it is safest read as aspirational identification rather than a literal
autobiographical manifesto. It nevertheless shows which concepts he selected to dramatise.

### April–early May: domestic intimacy and demand for clarity

The Sneg thread is no longer merely a game channel. It includes affectionate terms, saying he
missed her, invitations to watch things together, food and smoking commentary, late-night
shared viewing and the line “С тобой неплохо / Я бы даже сказал хорошо.”

On 8 May a small interaction displays the later epistemic pattern in miniature. He asked what
she was doing and what they would do, clarified that he was not forcing her, then named the
problem: “Просто неопределенность какая-то.” Ambiguity itself had become the aversive object.

### 24–25 May: the acute low

The strongest newly recovered evidence is the night of 24–25 May. He said:

- he was caught in a web and a very complicated game he could not handle;
- a situation involving money, an unsafe apartment, intoxicated people and a fight had
  reached an apex;
- he had extinguished a cigarette on himself to silence someone;
- he did not know what would happen the next day;
- actions he might take could lead to his death;
- he begged Sasha not to call because he was not in a position to take it.

This is the strongest retained candidate for the most *acutely dangerous* low. It outranks
December 2025 on immediate-risk language. The record cannot establish whether he intended
self-harm, meant exposure to external danger, was dramatising, or what happened next.
Therefore the correct statement is narrow: on that night he contemporaneously described
chaos, danger, overwhelm and possible death.

### June–July: withdrawal and the birthday message

June communication collapses. On 14 June he said he would stop bothering her but asked her to
tell him if something had happened because she was silent and not responding in game. On
13 July he sent one 728-character birthday message. It was not comic. It was formal,
restrained and elegiac:

- he wished her happiness and future help;
- said he still remembered their time together pleasantly;
- said he had thought about the message every day for half a month;
- called her “the greatest loss” of his life;
- called her a wonderful girl and good person.

That is not the voice of someone who had already converted the relationship into contempt.
The first response to loss was benediction plus self-erasure.

### 3 August: the reckoning

The final original sequence is more complicated than the later one-sided betrayal story. He
said he had withdrawn because he believed he disgusted her, then told her she had lost nothing
valuable. He said he had suffered silently for the first month and a half of summer. When the
exchange shifted, he said he was ashamed and did not know how to apologise for betraying her
trust; said some things should not be forgiven because otherwise they would happen again; and
then said he could no longer reciprocate and now felt only guilt.

This means the relationship cannot be reconstructed as only “Sasha betrayed and ghosted
him.” Her later overlapping attachments may still be supported by the 2023 Allen exchange,
but the original thread shows that Artiom also believed he had betrayed her trust and that
she was seeking some form of reconciliation or declaration to which he could no longer
respond. Her incoming words are absent, so the exact offence and request remain unknown.

### Account migration

The teen account falls from 4,946 July messages to 2,069 in the first nineteen days of August,
then almost disappears. The primary account begins on 11 August and becomes canonical.
This is a real account transition, not the end of social life.

## Speech development in the teen corpus

### Stable elements

- packeted rather than paragraph-first communication;
- rapid self-correction (“точнее”, “ой”, reversed claims);
- Russian with game English and occasional full English;
- teasing, insult and profanity as affiliative as well as aggressive tools;
- hyperbole and theatrical roleplay;
- moving from technical explanation to absurdity without a transition.

### Context-sensitive modes

1. **Mechanics mode:** sequential, causal, comparative, caveated.
2. **Peer mode:** clipped, profane, emoji-heavy and highly context-dependent.
3. **Operator mode:** rosters, rules, invitations, conflict mediation and group judgments.
4. **Vulnerable mode:** suddenly longer, formally structured, self-condemning and
   future-oriented.
5. **Performance mode:** copypasta, character voice, exaggerated masculinity or mock grandeur.

The LoRA implication is important: these are modes, not one average voice. Training all
Discord text as if it were one reply persona would collapse a raid copypasta, a private
apology and an authored mechanics explanation into the same behaviour.

## What did not change

Several later traits are already present before AI use:

- the drive to find the true mechanic rather than repeat received wisdom;
- status sensitivity and a wish to be the best;
- building identity through a coherent world;
- selected loyalty and attention to withdrawal;
- using humour and performance to create group atmosphere;
- turning mistakes into causal explanations and future rules;
- repeated attempts to leave a domain that had ceased to feel alive.

AI did not create these patterns. It made them easier to scale, verbalise and turn back on the
self.

## What changed

The major movement is from **socially embodied competence** to **abstracted, owned
sovereignty**.

In 2020–2021, proof came from people and a shared world: damage, raids, invitations, status,
friends and daily presence. In 2022, the cost of depending on one person's reciprocity became
painfully visible. From 2023 onward, more of the same need was transferred into products,
sales, companies, technical systems, appearance metrics, AI agents and finally memory
infrastructure—things whose state could be inspected and whose rules he could increasingly
author.

This is development, but also displacement. The later system is more powerful and durable;
the old world supplied spontaneous belonging that no private architecture can fully replace.
