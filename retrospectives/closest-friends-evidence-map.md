## How “closest” was inferred

Message count was used only as one signal. The ranking also considers:

- continuity across years and platforms;
- vulnerable disclosure and who receives unguarded material;
- reciprocal, two-sided contact where the archive permits it;
- shared offline life, travel and practical coordination;
- whether the relationship persists into 2025–26;
- whether the person is repeatedly used as a sounding board for real decisions;
- explicit affection or friendship language.

This distinction matters because the corpus contains several different friendships: a person
can be the most frequent correspondent, the deepest crisis confidant, the most active current
companion, or the most important group member without being all four.

## Evidence by person

### 1. Andrei Kavaliou = Bottle — longitudinal first place

The identity merge is high-confidence. The Discord DM labelled `bottle0_0` includes “Andrei
le Bouteille” and “Mr Andrew Bottle.” A pasted July 2025 Telegram exchange is explicitly with
Andrei Kavaliou, and Artiom addresses him directly as “Ало ботол.” This is not two separate
friends.

The Discord archive contains **14,757 account-sent messages** to Bottle over **457 active
days and 42 active months**, from August 2022 to February 2026. Only Definition/Anton's
deleted-account DM has a higher outgoing count. The correspondence moves far beyond WoW: relationships,
identity, countries, money, technology, university, daily observations and logistics all
appear. In November 2023 they planned and undertook the Berlin/Warsaw trip together. The
two-sided Telegram material pasted into ChatGPT in 2025–26 continues into clothing, teaching,
property, investing, work, visas and future travel. That breadth is the strongest evidence of
a friend functioning as an everyday co-thinker.

The friendship is expressed more through joint analysis, banter, schemes and mobility than
through sentimental declarations. That matches Artiom's general friendship style and should
not be mistaken for emotional distance between the two.

### 2. Serge Sotskyi — current social-life first place

The raw Instagram export contains **6,137 direct messages** in the two-sided Serge thread
from October 2023 to July 2026: 3,944 from Serge and 2,193 from Artiom before cleaning out
non-text events. Serge is also the most active participant in **Brotein Shake**, whose source

The affection is unusually explicit. On 27 March 2025 Artiom wrote, “I love you bro”; Serge
answered, “That's how much we love.” On 7 October he wrote, “Happy birthday brother.” Their

mutual group maintenance. Unlike several older Discord relationships, this one is both
current and visibly reciprocal.

Serge therefore outranks everyone except Andrei overall, and may be number one if “closest”
means who is most embedded in Artiom's present offline/social life rather than who has the
longest history.

### 3. Allen — continuous confidant and technical peer

### 4. Lino / Dima — remote old friend newly visible in full

The native Telegram export contains **11,363 bilateral records** with Lino: 4,695 outgoing,
6,660 incoming and eight service records, across 269 active days and 33 active months from
June 2023 to June 2026. The thread itself refers to a relationship of roughly ten years.

This is not mere feed-sharing. They exchange relationship failures, life philosophy, work,
war and military life, migration, bodies, money, gaming and ordinary updates. In October 2024
Lino revisited Artiom's old invitation to move to Britain and said he wanted to go somewhere

arrived. In May 2026 they discussed arranging their apparent first meeting in a decade through
an Elbrus trip; Lino called himself the “best friend” in the same conversation and expressed
genuine happiness that Artiom's life was going well.

The qualification is important: 1,349 messages in the thread are explicitly forwarded, and a
large portion of its surface traffic is political/news material and gaming humour. It is also
an almost entirely remote relationship. Those factors keep it below Allen's broader
cross-platform specialist-confidant role, but its longevity, reciprocity and personal content

## The crucial emotional qualification

On 9 June 2026 Artiom wrote that he is “pretty detached from almost everyone,” likes his
friends and understands why he needs them, but does not feel the expected sensation of
“companionship / brotherhood.” This must govern the interpretation.

The corpus therefore supports **behavioural closeness without conventional emotional fusion**.
Artiom's closest friendships are the people granted sustained access to his thought stream,
practical plans, jokes, travel, relationship material and unfinished ideas. He often enacts
friendship through availability, analysis, collaboration and shared experience rather than
through felt dependence or sentimental language. Serge is the notable partial exception
because the affection is explicit and reciprocal.

## Source limitations

- Discord preserves only Artiom's sent messages; incoming replies and deleted messages are
  absent.
- The canonical Instagram JSONL is a cleaned operator-message view. Two-sided counts above
  were checked against the immutable Instagram source archive.
- ChatGPT and Claude show what Artiom chose to analyse. A person mentioned often may be a
  problem or comparison target rather than a close friend.
- Pasted chats are evidence, but they are selected fragments. The native Telegram history is
  now present; frequency, reciprocity and content require deterministic cross-corpus
  reconciliation before the ranking is revised.
- Message volume cannot distinguish affection from gaming operations, conflict, copied text
  or a short burst of obsession. It was never used alone.
