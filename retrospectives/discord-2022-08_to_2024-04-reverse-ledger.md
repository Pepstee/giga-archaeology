# Discord reverse chronology — April 2024 to August 2022

**Written:** 19 July 2026  
**Direction:** backwards, beginning in April 2024  
**Source:** 83,779 account-sent Discord messages in `discord_messages_primary_20260719.jsonl`  
**Status:** first complete monthly reconstruction; awaiting Artiom's corrections

This is the Discord substrate beneath the broader characterisation. It deliberately begins
in April 2024 and walks backward one month at a time to 11 August 2022, the beginning of the
export. It records action, thought, emotional evidence and developmental movement before
trying to impose a single story on the period.

## Evidence rules

Discord exported only messages sent by the account. It did not export the incoming side of
DMs, deleted messages, or reliable authorship metadata. Therefore:

- **Direct** means the message is written as a personal claim and fits adjacent context.
- **Supported** means several messages or an externally legible action point the same way.
- **Provisional** means the action is likely but the missing incoming side matters.
- **Paste/performance** means the account transmitted the object, but authorship or
  endorsement is not established.

Pasted code is evidence. It shows what technical objects were in attention, what was being
tested or distributed, and—when accompanied by debugging and revision—what competence was
being developed. It does not establish that every pasted line was personally authored. The
same law applies to political forwards, lyrics, copypasta, AI reformulations, quotations and
messages written by someone else.

Emotional state below means *expressed or strongly evidenced state*, not a diagnosis or a
claim about every day. Message volume is context rather than a measure of importance.

## The backward ledger

### April 2024 — divided engagement: building, re-entry and institutional slippage

**What happened.** On 1 April Artiom was configuring a private gaming environment and

concern email had arrived because of Moodle inactivity and that somebody else would need to
request the postponement.

**What he was thinking.** The largest personal exchange, on 16 April, concerned ethnicity,
appearance, culture and chosen identity. He ordered a DNA test, challenged another person's
picture of him, and asked whether appearance and culture could really define him. He called
himself culturally Moldovan from Britain rather than Finnish, and preferred loud, dense
cities to an identity built around nature. The exchange is exploratory and combative rather
than a settled ethnic doctrine ([raw message](</Users/admin/Documents/giga-user/corpus/raw/discord_messages_primary_20260719.jsonl:76520>),
[question about definition](</Users/admin/Documents/giga-user/corpus/raw/discord_messages_primary_20260719.jsonl:76526>)).

**Emotional reading.** Energetic and intellectually engaged, but irregular. The record does
not support the earlier tidy picture of steady coursework. It supports simultaneous project
activity, late sleep, gaming re-entry, and avoidant or inconsistent institutional engagement.

**Movement.** Modern WoW and university work were both supposed to provide live difficulty
and progression. Yet the month also shows that knowing what deserves attention did not make
attention reliably governable. The central tension is not work versus leisure; it is intense
selective engagement versus weak compliance with low-charge obligations.

### March 2024 — choosing live systems over nostalgic mastery

**Census:** 651 messages; 619 text-bearing; 28 active days; 19 channels; 61.9% DMs.

**What happened.** On 4 March he invited classmates to free stand-up with Serge and Arian.
On 13 March, at 02:41, he said he should sleep earlier while arranging a house viewing with
Scarlett, estimating rent and utilities, seeking four residents, promising to attend lectures,
and asking what a LinkedIn session had covered. On 19 March he assembled proof-of-address
receipts to change his Blizzard region. On 22 March he chose gear through simulations and
stat weights rather than intuition. On 28 March he said he had withdrawn from his phone for
a few days.

The 31 March discussion is the month's centre. He regretted spending so long on the old
private server and wished he had moved to official WoW during Battle for Azeroth. In less than
two months, beginning without gear, knowledge or contacts, he had reached a high rating. He
liked that official play forced him to learn rather than repeat a solved routine. He argued
that a raid should not depend on a leader improvising in real time: roles, timing and responses
should be documented in advance.

**What he was thinking.** Feeling is a poor instrument where simulation and comparative data
exist. Progress means adaptation to a living system; dominance over a decaying, repeatedly
rewritten private-server meta is counterfeit significance. Leadership should be converted
from charisma and memory into prior documentation.

**Emotional reading.** Restless but hopeful. There is pride in transfer speed and novelty,
alongside sleep and attendance slippage. This is not an abandonment of gaming logic. It is
that logic being upgraded: current competition, measurement, documentation and learning.

**Movement.** The systems-builder outgrew the closed world in which he first became one. The
same demand—make the rules explicit, optimise against reality, do not stagnate—was beginning
to bridge game, student life and later work.

candidate high, not enough evidence for “happiest.”

**Movement.** After January's travel crisis, ordinary embodied life returned quickly. The
important change is not that ambition disappeared; it began being rationed across code,
university, social life and only the highest-yield form of gaming.

**Movement.** The month contains the whole later architecture in miniature: build an object,

**Emotional reading.** Mobile, intellectually performative and socially active, but tired and

**Movement.** The post-breakup distrust of July became a portable doctrine rather than a

**Movement.** September is the narrow bridge between July's relationship rupture and
October's university revival. It shows why source provenance matters: a dramatic resignation
in the raw account is not his event, while the quiet repeated claims about work and not

pride. It also contains explicit early gender essentialism—“you are a man, not a woman; use
logic, not feelings.” On 7 December a loot dispute made him worry less about the object than
about others doubting his integrity. On 9 December he gave a careful apology, distinguishing
intention, harm, evidence and future conduct. On 8 December he mentored a new raid leader on
interface, cooldowns, guilt and the value of a voice team.

On 11 December he produced an approximately two-thousand-character guide to active versus
effective DPS and ways the metric could be abused. On 13 December he explained that writing
the guide improved him because it forced research and produced instructions that solved
future combat problems in advance ([reflection](</Users/admin/Documents/giga-user/corpus/raw/discord_messages_primary_20260719.jsonl:32826>)).
On 15 December he calculated role and damage allocation. Sleep was poor around 25–26 December.

**What he was thinking.** Writing is not merely display; it is a cognitive machine. Research,
paraphrase and organisation create a reusable external mind. Reputation represents accumulated
time and effort. Teams require truth, repair and explicit procedure, though his “logic versus
feelings” gender binary was already present.

**Emotional reading.** Competent, responsible, perfectionistic and strained. He could be
injured by doubt and still produce a genuine apology rather than only defend himself.

**Movement.** This is one of the clearest origins of GIGA's mechanics. Long before AI agents,
he learned that documenting a procedure improves the author, coordinates other people and
pre-solves recurring decisions.

### November 2022 — the strongest affective low beneath a high-functioning exterior

**Census:** 8,103 messages; 7,644 text-bearing; 30 active days; 94 channels; 81.1% DMs.

On 14 November, however, he sent a private farewell-like sequence. At 09:05 he wrote, “If I
suddenly disappear,” thanked Oleg for two years and said he had been glad to know him

supports continuing existential bleakness, although its conversational context is incomplete.

**What he was thinking.** The public self could master and teach a system while the private
self saw almost no future. Gaming was both a real social institution and an avoidance device;
those truths are compatible.

**Emotional reading.** This is the best-supported candidate for the lowest affective point in

**Movement.** October's “most alive” mobility philosophy and November's near-total isolation

## What changed across these twenty-one months

### 1. The arena changed before the operating system did

In 2022 the dominant arena was the guild: exact plans, roles, status, governance, teaching,
reputation and archives. By late 2023 the old server had lost its claim to seriousness, but
the operator did not vanish. He applied the same mechanics to university groups, websites,
housing, travel and eventually the imagined “IRL guild.” The developmental change was not
from fantasy to reality. It was from one closed institution to several live, consequential
ones.

### 2. Documentation moved from tactic to identity

The December 2022 insight is foundational: writing a guide forces research, improves the
writer and pre-solves future decisions. By March–April 2024 he wanted raids documented in
advance and used simulations rather than feelings. Pasted code belongs in this trajectory:
selection, testing, alteration and distribution made external artefacts part of thought.
Later knowledge systems did not arrive from nowhere; they generalised a practice learned in
competitive coordination.

### 3. Isolation and social competence were simultaneous

November 2022 shows severe private isolation underneath one of the most socially dense and
technically productive periods in the archive. October–December 2023 then show strong
university sociability, travel, apology and care. “Introvert becoming social” is therefore too
simple. He could lead, teach and perform while profoundly alone; later he found more embodied
forms of belonging, but remained selective and defended.

### 4. Relationship thought hardened through a traceable sequence

April–May 2023 contains love, protection and the explicit wish for happiness rather than
mere sex. The July rupture was then processed as evidence, psychology, logic and principle.
The legitimate conclusion—do not tolerate coercion or disrespect—expanded into categorical
misogyny. Those beliefs were visible again in November–December 2023. The chronology neither
dismisses them as a temporary December 2025 mood nor validates them as true. It shows how a
specific injury was converted into a general doctrine and then retained.

### 5. “Do not stagnate” became the master developmental rule

The strongest positive self-description involved travel, work, partnership and winning
together outside comfort. By March 2023 he saw private-server rank as illusory. By October he
formally prioritised IRL plans. By March–April 2024 he preferred a current, adaptive game and
criticised nostalgia as regression. Mobility, new systems and steep learning curves are not
miscellaneous preferences; they repeatedly restore aliveness.

### 6. Exceptionalism became more explicit, not newly created

Shame before elite players, “strive for perfection,” top-rank comparisons and contempt for
easy realm-first status all predate university and sales. What changed was the reference
class. Rank inside a small private server stopped being credible proof, so the search for a
harder arena moved outward. This is growth when it selects real challenges; it becomes a
wound when only rare distinction can make ordinary life count.

## Paste and provenance corrections that materially change the biography

- The August 2022 school rant, January 2023 misogynistic story and November 2023 long story
  are copied/performed objects, not personal events.
- The March and September 2023 guild-resignation text is written in feminine grammar and was

## Provisional developmental stages

1. **Competitive operator and performed strategist (Aug–Oct 2022):** public-facing work,
   streaming, technical orchestration, cross-server networking, mobility philosophy.
2. **Hidden collapse under mastery (Nov 2022):** severe private isolation underneath dense
   online leadership and output.
3. **Procedural consolidation and institution-building (Dec 2022–Feb 2023):** guides,
   governance, teaching, guild construction and disappointment with collective reliability.
4. **External-life integration (Mar–Jun 2023):** old rank loses legitimacy; work, study,
   citizenship, body, home and relationship enter the same planning frame.
5. **Rupture and doctrinal hardening (Jul–Sep 2023):** relationship pain is translated into
   principle, distrust and categorical gender claims while work pulls activity offline.
6. **Embodied social reopening (Oct–Dec 2023):** university friendship, nightlife, travel,
   websites and the “IRL guild,” alongside continued sleep disorder and hardened beliefs.
7. **Mobile bridge and selective re-entry (Jan–Apr 2024):** border crisis, return, friendship,
   sex, coding, modern competitive play and anti-stagnation thought; institutional engagement
   remains inconsistent.

These stages are descriptive, not a ladder from worse to better. Skills, wounds and doctrines
cross their boundaries. The monthly evidence should be corrected by Artiom wherever memory
or incoming Discord context changes the story.
