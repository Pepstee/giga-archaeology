# Friendship types and functions

Date: 21 July 2026  
Scope: both ChatGPT exports, Claude, Instagram, Discord, and cross-corpus ledgers.  
Method: relationship form is inferred from conversational behaviour—not merely message
volume—including initiation, reciprocity, subject range, disclosure, shared offline action,
future orientation, affection, conflict, and the role assigned to each person. These are
descriptions of the relationships, not diagnoses of either participant.

## Core conclusion: a federated intimacy system

Artiom does not have one best friend who contains every mode of closeness. He distributes
the functions of friendship across people:

- **Andrei** receives the strategic and unfinished thinking self.
- **Serge** receives and sustains the playful, embodied, socially alive self.
- **Allen** holds the mastery history, specialist language, and difficult facts.
- **Joel** is attached to future action, travel, projects, and an adult bond still being made.
- **Oleg** was entrusted with the self at the point of existential collapse.
- **Definition / Anton** held the lonely, philosophical, combative shadow self in the
  deleted 2022–23 Discord DM and later reappears inside Svinarnik.
- **Brotein Shake** makes belonging physical; **Svinarnik** makes it linguistic and theatrical.

This is not an absence of friendship. It is **modular friendship**: each relationship is
real, but purpose-specific. The system supplies breadth, resilience, and low dependence. Its
cost is fragmentation: almost nobody sees all of Artiom, and no human necessarily has the
integrated access that the AI corpus now has.

## Andrei / Bottle: the companion-strategist with a Pygmalion shadow

Andrei is not principally an emotional-confession friend. He is the closest equivalent to a
**co-author of life strategy**. The relationship moved from a mastery alliance in WoW into a
general-purpose adult planning laboratory: travel, housing, property, money, work, language
study, women, countries, technology, and future possibilities. Berlin/Warsaw made the bond
physical, but its centre remains cognitive. The pair repeatedly simulate possible futures
and troubleshoot one another's present.

The intimacy is in the lack of ceremony. Andrei is allowed to see plans while they are still
half-formed; he is asked to test, compare, and improve them. That is deeper access than
sentimental language would indicate. He provides continuity between the guild self and the
adult architect self.

The danger is that Artiom sometimes stops relating to Andrei as an equal and starts treating
him as an intervention target. In July 2025 he explicitly wanted to use Socratic and other
techniques to “подвести его к пробуждению”; during the attempted intervention he reported
“Он перестал читать” (`corpus/transcripts/chatgpt_conversations.jsonl:16880` and `:16884`).
The clean reading is not the assistant's speculative psychology. It is behavioural: the
friendship tolerates mutual thinking, but strains when mutuality becomes diagnosis,
hierarchy, or covert pedagogy. Andrei is closest when he is a **co-architect**, not a pupil.

## Serge: the brother-of-play and carrier of social warmth

Serge is the strongest **companionship friendship** in the corpus. This bond is made of
frequency, rhythm, invitations, shared spaces, memes, student life, gym, nights, trips, and
group maintenance. Its emotional security is enacted rather than explained. “I love you
bro” and “Happy birthday brother” (`corpus/raw/instagram_20260717.jsonl:1205`, `:1834`) are
not isolated against a cold background; they name what the ongoing behaviour already does.

Using a greater-than-twelve-hour gap as a rough session boundary, Serge initiated 448 of 622
direct Instagram sessions, while Artiom initiated 174. Serge also produces the most messages
and starts the most sessions in Brotein Shake. This makes him more than the most frequent
friend: he is one of the people actively **keeping Artiom socially connected**. Artiom gives
him humour, interpretation, shared experiences, and unmistakable affection; Serge supplies
social momentum and low-demand continuity.

This friendship is emotionally lighter than Oleg's crisis role or Definition's historical
existential dialogue, but not shallower. Its special value is ease. The shadow is an
initiation asymmetry and a banter shield: Artiom may rely on Serge to keep the channel alive,
while neither needs to translate affection into sustained direct vulnerability. The
relationship should be understood as **secure everyday brotherhood**, even if Artiom does
not internally experience the mythic “brotherhood feeling.”

## Allen: the specialist brother and keeper of difficult knowledge

Allen is a **legacy friendship that successfully survived its original environment**. WoW,
technical competence, niche humour, and shared history remain the common language, but the
relationship continues into Korea, work, appearance, study, relationships, and current life.
The Sneg/Sasha disclosure places Allen inside a high-trust boundary: he can bring facts that
materially change Artiom's understanding of his own history.

Allen initiated roughly twice as many direct Instagram sessions as Artiom (250 versus 121
under the same twelve-hour rule). Like Serge, he is not just passively available; he helps
preserve continuity. Unlike Serge, he is not embedded in the current local social world.
He functions more like a **reliable remote node**: no emotional ceremony is required, the
shared protocol still works, and serious material can pass through it.

Its limitation is that intimacy often travels sideways—through technical systems, lore, or
other people's relationship facts—rather than through direct reports of need. It can preserve
an old mastery identity and an old grievance-world. Its gift is durable recognition: Allen
knows the system Artiom came from and remains connected to the person who left it.

## Joel: the expedition partner and deliberately growing friendship

Joel is not yet an ancient confidant; he is a **chosen future friend**. Direct contact is
nearly balanced, and Artiom initiated more of the 96 rough Instagram sessions (56 versus
Joel's 40). At Christmas 2025 Artiom explicitly said he was grateful to have Joel as a friend
and hoped the bond would grow; Joel answered affirmatively. The corpus then connects the
friendship to travel, nights out, university, flights, group plans, and possible trading.

That makes Joel unusually important developmentally. Andrei and Allen connect back to the
mastery/guild past; Joel represents friendship made through **adult joint action**. The bond
does not rely on a lost world. It is being built by repeated voluntary investment.

The shadow appears when Artiom frames Joel hierarchically—wanting to “MOG” a table, deciding
how to elevate Joel in front of his girlfriend, or approaching him as someone whose trajectory
can be organised. Care and management become entangled. Joel can become a genuine expedition
partner only if help does not silently establish rank.

## Oleg / Bebop: the respected confessor and witness at the abyss

Oleg occupies a narrow but profound function. On 14 November 2022 Artiom began with “If I
suddenly disappear,” thanked him for two years, and disclosed family estrangement,
psychological illness, no visible future, isolation, and shame—material he said he had never
told anyone. Oleg's distinct role is therefore not everyday presence but **existential
witness**.

The trust seems to come from a combination of distance, competence, loyalty, and symbolic
authority. A respected online friend can sometimes receive truths that feel impossible to
place inside ordinary offline relationships. Oleg was safe enough to hold the unsocialised
version of the crisis.

The limitation is visible in the form of the disclosure: it was abrupt and farewell-like,
closer to opening an emergency vault than developing a sustained practice of mutual
vulnerability. Oleg is evidence that Artiom can disclose radically. He is not evidence that
Artiom routinely lets others accompany him through pain.

## Definition / Anton: the shadow twin and midnight philosopher

The formerly unresolved DM is the strongest historical **existential sparring friendship**.
The operator identifies the deleted participant as Definition/Anton, and the internal
evidence makes the merge high-confidence. Inside this one DM Artiom directly calls the other
person “Антон” and “Антонимыш” (`corpus/raw/discord_messages_primary_20260719.jsonl:19361`),
“La Definition” (`:29242`), “definition of success” (`:55681`), “Definition of Boar”
(`:55885`), and “Антон Торрентинко” (`:59161`). A December 2022 message also refers to their
“свинарник” (`:34323`). These independent identifiers are much stronger than the earlier
speculation that the account might be Bilor.

The DM's
16,422 outgoing messages combine continuous banter with ambition, masculinity, isolation,
relationships, mortality, and meaning. “Мы словно знаем друг друга всю жизнь” and “Я не хочу
терять друга такой глупой смертью” (`corpus/raw/discord_messages_primary_20260719.jsonl:29197`,
`:58836`) show both recognition and real concern.

This person appears to have been a shadow twin: someone with whom intimacy could be disguised
as argument, insult, theory, and shared alienation. That form allowed maximum cognitive
exposure without requiring conventional softness. It may also have reinforced the bleakest
parts of each worldview. Because the account disappears in 2023 and the identity is
later recovered through the 2025 Svinarnik material, this is not a vanished stranger but an
old intense dyad transformed into a group-bound relationship. The continuity changes the
meaning: Svinarnik did not introduce Definition; it **contained and redistributed an old
one-to-one bond**.

That transformation also clarifies the later hostility. In November 2025 Artiom wrote, “В
Антоне нет никакой ценности вне чата” (`corpus/transcripts/claude_conversations.jsonl:3040`).
Whatever its momentary anger, this is the opposite of the 2022–23 relationship, where Anton
clearly had substantial one-to-one value. By late 2025 the person had been reduced to a role
inside the arena. Their rupture was not principally caused by insufficient outreach; it was
caused by a formerly intimate sparring bond hardening into rivalry, status conflict, and
group-mediated interpretation.

## The groups: two different replacements for the guild

### Brotein Shake — the guild made physical

Brotein Shake is an **offline guild or expedition crew**. It performs many functions that
WoW guilds once performed—ritual, roles, teasing, coordination, shared missions, status, and
belonging—but anchors them in travel, pubs, sport, study, careers, and actual places. Serge is
the social heart; Joel is a planner and co-engine; Artiom is often the analyst, curator, and
broadcaster of plans, city rankings, lessons, and achievements; Alvaro is a practical comic
connector; Lorenc is more peripheral in the retained data.

This is a major developmental success: belonging has crossed from the screen into embodied
adult life. Its risk is that the group becomes an audience and ranking arena. Long achievement
reports, looks/dating comparison, racialised or sexual banter, and social performance can
make belonging contingent on being impressive. The crew is healthiest when it does things
together, not when it merely watches and scores its members.

### Svinarnik — the guild converted into language

Svinarnik is a **semiotic laboratory and theatre ensemble**. The members co-create personas,
running bits, post-ironic language, predictions, and verbal combat. Definition functions as
an administrative/verbal rival, Anatoliy as contrarian-absurdist, Snegovik as comic
accelerant, and Artiom as analyst/director—especially when he imports excerpts into AI and
asks for personality, intelligence, synergy, and reply predictions.

Its intimacy is cultural: to understand the language is to belong. Its danger is
datafication. Once friends are primarily treated as characters to rank, predict, and model,
analysis can replace encounter. Constant irony also makes unambiguous care unusually
expensive. Definition's identified 2022–23 DM now shows that at least one Svinarnik
relationship had considerably deeper bilateral history than the selected 2025 Telegram
excerpts alone revealed. **Update, 22 Jul 2026:** the native Telegram export has now landed;
later group dynamics still require a dedicated chronological and alias-reconciliation pass.

## Development over time

1. **2022–23 — guild kinship and disguised vulnerability.** Friendship was organised around
   competence, lore, hierarchy, and extremely dense DMs. Intimacy entered through problem
   solving, banter, mastery, crisis, and philosophical combat: Andrei, Allen, Oleg, and
   Definition each held a different fragment.
2. **Late 2023–24 — the bridge into physical life.** Berlin/Warsaw with Andrei and university,
   study, gym, stand-up, and local life with Serge show the online-guild template being
   translated into real-world companionship.
3. **2025 — two successor guilds.** Brotein Shake embodied belonging through activities and
   travel; Svinarnik turned belonging into shared language and psychological theatre. At the
   same time, Artiom increasingly submitted conversations to AI for analysis, moving from
   participant toward participant-observer and sometimes social engineer.
4. **2026 — active network, subjective detachment.** The relationships continue, but Artiom
   writes that he likes his friends and understands why he needs them without feeling the
   expected “companionship / brotherhood” (`corpus/transcripts/chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:12301`).
   The network is behaviourally rich but internally conceptualised as functional and
   non-binding.

## What friendship means to Artiom

Artiom's basic friendship verb is not **to confide**. It is **to build alongside**. He bonds
through a shared object: a game, trip, city, idea, course, group, system, joke, plan, woman,
or future. The friend is most legible when there is something between the two people to
inspect or do.

That produces several genuine strengths:

- unusually durable long-distance ties;
- loyalty across platform and life-stage changes;
- high tolerance for intellectual intensity and blunt truth;
- the ability to create shared worlds and move some of them offline;
- tenderness that is real precisely because it appears inside a generally unsentimental style.

It also produces the recurring deformation:

- the friend becomes a function, project, witness, audience, case study, or status mirror;
- understanding the person is confused with being entitled to steer them;
- AI-assisted interpretation can create certainty without reciprocal checking;
- direct need is displaced into advice, joking, planning, or spectacular one-off disclosure;
- maintaining autonomy becomes more important than letting anyone become indispensable.

The deepest distinction is therefore not “real friends versus shallow friends.” It is
**relationship versus management**. The friendships are most alive when Artiom joins Serge's
play, travels with Andrei, makes plans with Joel, or exchanges lore with Allen. They become
thinnest when the other person is reduced to someone to awaken, elevate, predict, classify,
or use as an audience.

## Is insufficient outreach threatening the friendships?

There is a real but bounded maintenance asymmetry. It does not currently look like wholesale
social withdrawal.

Using a greater-than-twelve-hour gap as a rough Instagram session boundary:

| Relationship | All retained sessions initiated by Artiom | Last 90 export-days | Last 30 export-days |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Serge | 174 / 622 (28%) | 26 / 66 (39%) | 6 / 19 (32%) |
| Allen | 121 / 371 (33%) | 17 / 61 (28%) | 9 / 19 (47%) |
| Joel | 56 / 96 (58%) | 13 / 30 (43%) | 1 / 5 (20%) |

The recent direct figures understate shared contact. In the last 30 export-days Artiom also
wrote 175 messages in Brotein Shake and initiated two of its seven rough sessions. Serge and
Joel therefore still encountered him regularly even when they opened most direct threads.
This is not the signature of friends being abandoned.

The genuine risk is subtler: **private relationships can gradually become group-only or
other-initiated**. Serge and Allen have historically carried more of the reopening burden;
Joel's relationship, previously led more by Artiom, has also tilted toward Joel recently.
Group presence preserves familiarity but does not fully replace being individually chosen.
The earlier claim that Andrei could not be measured comparably is now obsolete: the 22 Jul
native Telegram landing contains 49,990 bilateral records with him. A full reconciled pass is
still required because Discord remains one-sided and aliases must be joined. The latest
substantial previously retained two-sided example, on
11 June 2026, is initiated by Andrei (“ну че челлоид”; then “ты не спился?”) before he brings
his mother's visa problem (`corpus/transcripts/chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:12494`).
It confirms active trust but does not support treating Bottle as the one friendship Artiom
definitely maintains himself.

A proportionate maintenance floor would be:

- initiate one meaningful private exchange each week, rotating among the core friends;
- make one concrete call or in-person plan with each core friend roughly once a month;
- sometimes contact them with no achievement, problem, analysis, or request attached;
- follow up on something they previously said, which signals that the person—not merely the
  conversation topic—was retained.

This should remain a floor, not another optimisation dashboard. Friendship is weakened by
chronic passivity, but it can also be weakened when care becomes monitored performance.

## Final classification

Artiom's social world is an **archipelago of specialised brotherhoods**:

- Andrei is the **mind-brother**.
- Serge is the **life-brother**.
- Allen is the **craft-and-history brother**.
- Joel is the **future-and-action brother**.
- Oleg is the **abyss witness**.
- Definition / Anton is the **shadow brother**, later converted into a group rival.

The next developmental step is not necessarily to find a superior new friend. It is to allow
one or two existing friendships to become less specialised: initiate without needing a
project, disclose before a crisis, ask rather than diagnose, receive rather than manage, and
permit affection that has no instrumental purpose.
