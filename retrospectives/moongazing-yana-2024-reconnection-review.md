# Moongazing / Yana: 2024 reconnection review

Date: 22 July 2026  
Source: native Telegram Desktop export plus the operator's January 2026 retrospective account.

## Identity and scope

Moongazing is Yana, the former online girlfriend from 2023. The native Telegram thread contains
2,681 ordinary messages from 27 May to 1 August 2024: 1,289 outgoing and 1,392 incoming, with
2,525 text-bearing records across 41 active days. It is not the original relationship; it is the
post-breakup reconnection. Every one of seven sessions following at least 72 hours of silence was
opened by Yana.

## Sequence

### Re-entry through the deleted archive

Yana reopened contact after noticing that Artiom's side of an old Discord conversation had been
deleted. She began by asking why, then asked him not to remain silent. Both admitted that they were
not indifferent. She had checked Raider.io during the March Moscow attack because she feared he
might have been there. The first long conversation moved immediately from digital traces to trust,
the breakup, family illness and what each had retained.

### Pursuit–withdrawal resumes

Yana repeatedly sought clarity, responsiveness and evidence that she mattered. Artiom repeatedly
distinguished lack of initiative from “ignoring,” called them internet acquaintances, and argued
that he did not check on people closer to him either. She rejected the attempt to reset their
history: former partners could not become ordinary internet acquaintances merely by naming them so.

The old conflict structure returned in a changed form. She treated ambiguity and silence as
relationship acts requiring explanation. He treated requests for explanation as prosecutorial
“разборки” and defended himself through semantics, distance and reduced obligation. She could be
relentless and reassurance-seeking; he could be teasing, evasive and deliberately difficult to read.

### Emotional re-entry

By July they were exchanging dreams, gaming lore, life updates, jealousy jokes and accounts of the
year apart. Yana retained old voice messages and described listening to one during loneliness as
finding an old comfortable sweater. She said long discussion with him was one of the best feelings
in the world. She repeatedly asked not merely what had happened in his year, but how he had felt.

This shows both strong attachment and unusual analytic persistence. She closely tracked
contradictions and repeatedly distinguished factual biography from interior experience. Her
weakness was inability to disengage from an attachment she already knew was unsafe; she kept using
clarification as a route back into closeness.

### Sexual escalation and final rupture

On 28–29 July the thread became explicitly sexual. Yana participated and initially framed a simple
sexual exchange as acceptable. Artiom then added affectionate and quasi-relational cues: possessive
pet names, intense praise, interest in ordinary conversation, domestic imagery and repeated
reassurance. Yana's distress was not principally that he desired her sexually. It was that the
surrounding behaviour encouraged her to understand the encounter as emotionally meaningful.

When his tone shifted, she said she was crying, asked him to explain, and contrasted being called an
internet acquaintance with being called “mine.” She identified a recurring defensive mechanism:
he appeared to fear being abandoned and therefore preferred to abandon first, explaining his own
attachment away through logic. She called him the love of her life and said ordinary friendship was
not possible.

Artiom ultimately said he desired her but wanted no relationship, did not need her trust, and would
repeat the conduct even if she considered it use. She said she hoped he would one day love someone,
expressed disappointment, and asked him not to write. The native thread ends there.

## Assessment of Yana

Yana appears emotionally intense, abandonment-sensitive, highly reassurance-seeking and prone to
turning ambiguity into prolonged interrogation. She had difficulty accepting relational distance
and repeatedly reopened contact. These traits could feel pressuring and exhausting, and they match
the conflict pattern documented in 2023.

She was also articulate, observant and substantially correct about the 2024 contradiction. She did
not object to sexuality as such; she objected to receiving emotional signals that exceeded the
stated arrangement. She disclosed her mental state, asked for direct answers and finally set a clear
boundary. The evidence does not support treating her as a simple manipulator or narcissistic
villain.

## Assessment of Artiom's role

The 2023 relationship remains a mutually destructive incompatibility. The 2024 reconnection is more
asymmetrical. Artiom controlled availability, alternated warmth and distance, used flirtation and
jealousy ambiguity, escalated sexual access, and denied that the relational framing created
obligations. His later January 2026 account states that he knew what he was doing: recurring sex was
the preferred outcome, revenge for past harm the fallback, he enjoyed the performance, and he knew
Yana did not understand. That retrospective claim converts a plausible in-the-moment ambiguity into
admitted instrumentalisation, while remaining a self-report rather than independent proof of every
internal motive at the time.

## Developmental significance

The pair reverses positions across the two episodes. In 2023 Artiom experienced himself as the
controlled, accused partner and converted vulnerability into prosecution. In 2024 he held the
distance and converted intimacy into control. The common element is difficulty tolerating uncertain,
mutual vulnerability. When exposed, he either proves a case or controls the terms.

Moongazing is therefore not a candidate for the friend ranking. She is a major former attachment
and one of the clearest records of the transition from defensive moral certainty to deliberate
instrumentalisation of intimacy. The later willingness to name the conduct accurately is evidence
of insight, not retroactive repair.
