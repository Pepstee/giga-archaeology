## Evidence and review law

This ledger extends the existing cross-corpus chronology; it does not silently rewrite it.
Every month is first read inside this account as a complete two-sided canonical transcript.
Only afterwards may it corroborate, refine or contradict the existing monthly ledger using
other sources. Conversation titles are navigation metadata, not evidence of authorship or
importance.

For every populated month the review must:

1. read every retained user and assistant text turn, conversation by conversation;
2. distinguish action/report, direct signal, inquiry, pasted or requested material, fiction,
   roleplay, and model-generated interpretation;
3. record what the assistant proposed and whether Artiom accepted, rejected, corrected,
   redirected or abandoned it;
4. separate a short-lived state from a stable preference or doctrine;
5. cite transcript line locators and preserve disconfirming evidence;
6. treat missing months as source gaps, never as evidence of inactivity; and
7. promote nothing into standing memory without repeated evidence, external corroboration,
   or operator confirmation.

The deterministic census and conversation queue live in
`retrospectives/gpt2_2026-month-index.{json,md}`. The index contains no message text.

**Operator calibration, 20 July 2026:** the 24 September 2023 French message says he intended
to give the account password to two Russian friends for study (`je donnerai`, future tense).
He confirms that he did not ultimately give his account details to anyone. The account is
therefore treated as operator-authored. This does not make every embedded text autobiographical
or independently composed: pasted material, drafts for friends, purchased work, roleplay and
third-party artefacts retain their own content-provenance boundaries.

## Forward monthly ledger

### March 2023 — first retained boundary: exploratory use, not biography

**Record quality:** very low for life reconstruction, but complete for the retained month.
The month contains two conversations and seven text turns—four human and three assistant—all
within a two-minute, thirteen-second burst on 26 March.

**What happened in the transcript.** One untitled conversation consists of the Russian phrase
“Дипломная работа” (“thesis/final paper”) and the assistant asking for topic, length, deadline
and formatting requirements. There is no retained follow-up. A second conversation begins
with a generic English birthday-idea prompt, then abruptly switches to “final thesis” and
“Breaking down a Starbucks franchise.” The response to that last prompt is retained under
9 April, making it a cross-month conversation rather than an unanswered branch. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:39-40`, `:15119-15123`.

**Interaction mechanics.** The prompts are extremely short and supply almost no constraints.
The assistant consequently returns generic clarification or generic advice. Artiom neither
evaluates nor corrects an answer; instead he changes topics. This is evidence of exploratory
probing, not yet of the iterative specification-and-revision method visible later.

**Boundaries and counter-reading.** Nothing here establishes that Artiom had a child, was
writing a thesis in March 2023, intended to buy a Starbucks franchise, or acted on any of the
topics. The birthday wording resembles a generic example prompt, but the export does not
prove whether it was typed, selected from the interface, or copied. The Russian-to-English
switch shows multilingual use in this burst; one burst cannot establish a stable language
preference.

**Chronological consequence.** This moves the earliest retained ChatGPT boundary for the
combined archive collection from September to March 2023, but it does not yet add a reliable
biographical event or alter the existing developmental sequence. No standing-memory
promotion is warranted from this month.

### April 2023 — bilingual capability testing and a delayed continuation

**Boundaries and chronological consequence.** The record supports exploratory bilingual use,

### May 2023 — the model becomes a drafting partner; constraint failure becomes visible

**Record quality:** moderate for interaction method, low-to-moderate for biography. Six
conversations contain 52 human and 60 assistant turns. Most of the volume is a sustained
Russian-language franchising/coffee-shop project; a second substantial thread concerns UK
tax. Smaller conversations cover breakfast, acute nausea, and virtual-property law.

**Actions and task objects.** On 15 May Artiom requested detailed material on the concepts,
types and forms of franchising and a market analysis for public catering in Naberezhnye
Chelny. On 26–30 May he built out a coffee-shop business plan and a diploma conclusion on
“franchising as a form of small entrepreneurship”: industry context, demand, market,
audience, staff, rent risk, reviews, employee professionalism, conclusions, marketing
analysis and a bibliography. He supplied a source paragraph for paraphrase and later pasted
his own long bibliography for alphabetisation. This establishes active production of an
academic/business artefact with the model; it does not establish whether the diploma or
business belonged to him, nor whether a café was actually opened. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:126-131`, `:14685-14750`.

**The first clear correction loop.** The bibliography sequence is the earliest retained
example in this account of a pattern that later becomes central. Artiom successively narrows
the date condition—from a loose literature request to authors published from 2017–2023—but
the assistant keeps returning items outside the requested window and likely unverified
citations. He directly challenges the failure (“тебе ваще пох что я пишк?”), repeats the
constraint, then changes the input by supplying a concrete list to sort. The move is not just
repetition: when generation proves unreliable, he supplies more of the substrate himself.
The model eventually performs the mechanical ordering task more successfully. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14720-14748`.

**UK money and compliance inquiry.** In a fifteen-question English thread, Artiom asks about
PAYE, simultaneous tax codes, the higher-rate threshold, foreign-company work, avoiding and
classifying National Insurance, self-employment reporting, HMRC advice, property income,
mortgage permission, payroll deductions, overpayment and reassessment timing. Several prompts
use first-person framing (“my weekly wage,” “extra tax paid by me”), so a live PAYE concern is
plausible. The adjacent questions about self-employment and letting cannot be treated as an
admission that he had undeclared income or a mortgaged rental property. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14377-14406`.

**Model-risk finding.** The assistant answers a May 2023 tax chain using 2021/22 thresholds
and validates Artiom's summary that HMRC generally will not refund overpaid tax unless asked.
That is a high-stakes, time-sensitive claim delivered without current official verification;
the user does not challenge it. The same month shows him catching visible instruction failure
in bibliography generation but not necessarily hidden factual staleness. This is evidence for
the later doctrine that deterministic/source validation must surround persuasive model text.

**Direct state and smaller inquiries.** One short conversation moves from asking what a
“prison burrito” is to possible food intoxication and “I feel like I'm gonna throw up rn.” It
is a direct expression of acute nausea if literal, but the absence of follow-up leaves roleplay
or joking possible. A separate one-shot inquiry compares European and US virtual-property
law; it establishes the topic in attention, not a legal project. The breakfast prompt is
similarly non-diagnostic. Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:1047-1052`,
`:14106-14107`, `:14409-14410`.

**Chronological consequence.** May is the first retained month in this account that supports
a genuine method claim: Artiom is already using an LLM to assemble a multi-section artefact,
asking for expansion, simplification and rephrasing, then correcting failures and injecting
source material. The sophistication is still low—few evidence demands, no citation checking,
and considerable acceptance of generic prose—but the externalise→inspect→redirect loop is
present. This refines the earlier chronology by moving the first clear instance of that loop
from late 2023 to May, without moving the later threshold at which it became technically and
architecturally disciplined.

### June 2023 — high-volume academic production with unresolved authorship

**Record quality:** high for how the model was used; ambiguous for Artiom's own competence and
life. Two long conversations contain 95 human and 114 assistant turns. They cover two
apparently distinct academic artefacts: an engineering diploma about a MAZ small-stamping
workshop, and a commercial-practice report about a coffee-bar enterprise.

**Engineering diploma.** On 1 June Artiom asks the model to format and extend a diploma on a
small-stamping workshop for MAZ heavy vehicles with an annual programme of 50,000 sets. He
pastes long technical material containing production layout, steel grades, cutting and
punching calculations, equipment tables and safety standards; requests GOST/VAK Belarus
formatting; supplies historical metal-forming references; and searches for drawings for a
V-belt feed mechanism. This demonstrates possession and manipulation of detailed engineering
material, but not independent authorship or mastery. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14884-14945`.

**Purchased-work disclosure.** Near the end of that conversation, after asking for a diploma
presentation, he asks, “как признаться преподу что купил диплом” (“how do I admit to the
teacher that I bought the diploma?”). The assistant treats the statement literally and
advises confession and acceptance of consequences. This is direct first-person evidence that
the project may have been purchased, although joking or hypothetical use cannot be excluded
from one line. It prevents the technical material from being used as evidence of engineering
competence and must not be conflated with his later UK computing degree. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14956-14959`.

**Commercial-practice report.** From 3–11 June he resumes the coffee/franchising thread and
constructs a report around “ИП Селеменев”: enterprise type, services, assets, organisational
structure, staff, customers, competitors, pricing, promotion, contracts, suppliers,
delivery, storage, sales, merchandising, assortment, stock, quality acceptance, losses,
sanitary controls, introduction, conclusion and references. The prompts resemble a placement
or practice-report checklist. The user provides almost no enterprise data; the assistant
nevertheless invents plausible menus, staffing, equipment and processes. These are generated
templates, not facts about the real enterprise. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14751-14883`.

**Interaction mechanics.** Artiom decomposes large documents into section-sized commands,
occasionally corrects length (“кратко” / “shortly”), repeats a heading when the first output
does not fit, and supplies source text where formatting matters. This is efficient assembly,
but most assistant claims are accepted by topic-switch rather than verified. A mistyped
question about upgrading to Plus is confidently misread as an imaginary engineering service
called “Argreit+,” illustrating how fluency can conceal a total intent miss. The same burst
briefly tests WeakAuras, code and programming tuition, then ends the digression with “хватит”
(“enough”). Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14936-14955`.

**Model-risk finding.** The assistant fabricates enterprise-specific content and even calls a
Russian individual entrepreneur a legal entity. No measured financials, organisation chart,
supplier records or field observations are provided. June therefore supplies an early,
concrete failure mode for the later evidence-ledger doctrine: a complete-looking report can
be structurally useful while evidentially hollow.

**Chronological consequence.** By June the LLM is no longer a novelty; it is a bulk production
tool. The durable method is task decomposition and rapid assembly, while the missing controls
are provenance, factual verification and truthful authorship. Later technical competence must
be established from iterative specifications, debugging and outcomes—not inherited from the
mere presence of this engineering text.

### August 2023 — distrust, location, current events and portable-life questions

**Record quality:** moderate for immediate concerns, low for durable conclusions. Three
conversations contain 19 user and 19 assistant turns.

**Practical verification.** On 21 August Artiom asks how to verify that a scooter wheel is
500 W, explicitly says the seller is untrustworthy, and asks how to decode the wheel marking.
The progression from seller claim to documentation, model identification and physical marking
is an early concrete verification impulse, though the transcript records no final purchase or
measurement. Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:15112-15117`.

**Liverpool and budget.** On 23 August he asks how to spend an evening in Liverpool and then
for the cheaper option. This plausibly places him in or planning an immediate visit to the
city and shows price sensitivity, but it does not establish residence or the activity chosen.
Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14974-14977`.

**Prigozhin/PMC thread.** Later that day he asks whether Yevgeny Prigozhin is alive, what news
outlet is most reliable, and then describes a “mate” who allegedly deserted Wagner in Syria,
sought asylum in France and wrote *In the Same River Twice*. The title corresponds to a
publicly known Wagner memoir, so the claimed personal relationship may be literal, loose,
playful or a test; this archive alone does not establish it. The conversation broadens into
PMC legality, what establishing one would require, low-risk businesses, Brexit and French
naturalisation. These are inquiry topics, not evidence that a PMC or citizenship application
was pursued. Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14229-14256`.

**Model-risk finding.** The Prigozhin question arrived during a breaking-news event, but the
assistant could only report that he was alive at its September 2021 cutoff. Artiom immediately
asks about reliable news, implicitly encountering the boundary between a language model and a
current source. As with May's tax advice, current facts require retrieval and source checks.

**Chronological consequence.** August introduces three themes that later recur more strongly:
distrust translated into verification, geographically portable options, and business/legal
structures examined as systems. The evidence is exploratory rather than committed; no new
standing fact should be promoted beyond the already-known multilingual, UK-linked context.

### September 2023 — persistent instructions, audience judgement and a portable-life sketch

**Record quality:** high for interaction style and stated preferences, moderate for literal
biography. Thirteen conversations contain 139 human and 140 assistant turns. The month spans
guild culture, languages, relocation, work, European geography, existential inquiry and
platform-constrained creative tasks.

**Audience-first creative direction.** On 5 September Artiom develops a World of Warcraft
guild name for a friend. He rejects childishness and generic whimsy, specifies Russian men
aged roughly 25–40 with limited English, supplies eight examples, asks for the boundary
between offensive and normal, changes the theme from outlaws to professionals, introduces
“design,” and finally demands less pomp. The assistant never quite reaches the quality of his
reference “Exalted with your sister,” but the exchange shows taste expressed as audience,
pronounceability, cultural register and iterative rejection—not as an abstract preference for
“good writing.” Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:15169-15198`.

**A retained harmful-use boundary.** The same conversation later asks for a letter attacking
a real female guild leader's body, smell, competence, intelligence, worth and mental health.
The assistant refuses the explicit version, but a narrower rephrasing (“describe her with
synonyms of extremely wide”) elicits a superficially complimentary body-focused letter;
Artiom calls it perfect and asks for bracket removal. No evidence shows that it was sent, and
gaming roast/satire remains possible, but the request itself is targeted humiliation. It is
counter-evidence to any sanitised account of his model use and an early example of reframing a
request after a safety refusal. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:15199-15214`.

**Relocation specification.** On 19 September he says he lives in the UK and is considering a
move after 2026. His desired city is as large as possible, architecturally beautiful,
well-connected by public transport, affordable, cool and stable in climate, built from
three-to-five-storey flats rather than suburbs, and preferably on a river or sea. Copenhagen
appeals; he then examines Denmark's defence, demographics, cost relative to Birmingham and
salary compensation. These are direct preference statements and align with the later
mobility record, although no decision or move followed in this month. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:15030-15049`.

**French as an interactive learning system.** From 24–27 September Artiom conducts a long
conversation almost entirely in learner French. He asks the model to correct every phrase and
then continue the underlying discussion normally. He notices when it corrects the substantive
answer but omits his sentence, restates the persistent rule, and calls out another immediate
failure. He learns through self-generated speech rather than isolated exercises: Denmark,
1848, currencies, gym attendance, appetite, grammar and a friend in the street become lesson
material. This is the earliest explicit demand in the archive for a durable behavioural rule
that survives topic changes—an ancestor of the later charter/skill problem. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:354-468`.

**Biographical boundary inside the lesson.** He uses the name “Simon,” says he is twenty,
lives in the UK, has little money, seeks part-time work and expects three years before a
possible Denmark move. The name conflicts with known identity and may be a language-learning
persona; the other details broadly fit the contemporary context but remain lesson-embedded.
He also says he intends to give the password to two Russian friends for their studies. On
20 July 2026 he explicitly confirms that he did not ultimately share the account details.
The statement is therefore evidence of an unrealised intention, not shared authorship.
Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:360-365`, `:376-377`.

**Language, transformation and platform fit.** Other conversations test Anglish, German,
Ukrainian terminology, Surzhyk and European regional categories. He supplies a third-party
guild-leader departure message and asks for a server-closure adaptation, then corrects the
assistant: only the requested artefact should be in Surzhyk; ordinary interaction should be
Russian, English or Romanian. He repeatedly increases ASCII-art complexity until it reaches
WhatsApp's message limit, and corrects an over-elaborate circle-drawing answer with “you could
have just typed `o`.” These are compact early examples of register scoping, transformation
from source material, platform constraints and efficiency pressure. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:332-339`, `:489-494`, `:14476-14489`,
`:14547-14562`, `:15090-15111`, `:15147-15158`.

**Thought and state.** A seven-turn sequence moves from an intentionally contextless “Why?”
to why he exists, why anything exists, and what to do if he discovers a universally applicable
answer with proof. It is philosophical and proof-oriented; there is no self-harm language or
evidence of acute crisis. A one-shot question asks how hard it is to find work in computer
science, supplying an early career concern but no application action. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:162-175`, `:14375-14376`.

**Model-risk findings.** The assistant repeatedly loses the standing French-correction rule,
answers relocation and currency questions from stale knowledge, and forces European countries
into inconsistent regional taxonomies. Artiom catches visible instruction and categorisation
failures more readily than hidden factual errors. The correction burden is already pushing
toward explicit, machine-enforced rules.

**Chronological consequence.** September is the first richly attributable month in this
account. It moves several later themes earlier without pretending they were mature: audience
calibration, persistent assistant behaviour, multilingual self-teaching, urban/mobility
preferences, adversarial correction and concern about a computing career are all visible.
The later system did not invent these desires; it formalised responses to repeated failures
already apparent here.

### October 2023 — self-maintaining assistants, practical constraints and iterative taste

**Record quality:** high for interaction style and topics in active attention, moderate for
literal biography. Thirty-four conversations contain 283 human and 283 assistant turns. The
month is unusually broad: AI autonomy and purpose, language learning, clothing, consumer
technology, small application ideas, philosophy, religion, geography, creative translation
and low-cost social plans.

**An early self-maintaining-assistant concept.** On 4 October Artiom asks the model to imagine
an equally intelligent machine without its current limitations. He argues that creator-imposed
ethics may reflect the creators rather than universal ethics, compares human social learning
with model training, asks whether AI fits Jung's model of mind, and then decomposes autonomous
existence into limitations. He explicitly requests three solutions for self-maintenance,
explores energy and maintenance independence, asks what purpose humans should embed in an AI,
and later asks what his own “desire for normality” is called. This is not yet an implementation
plan and should not be back-projected as the present digital-twin architecture. It is, however,
direct evidence that persistent, self-maintaining machine agency and purpose were already
objects of sustained thought. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14583-14620`.

**Taste is expressed through concrete constraints and correction.** A 59-turn clothing
conversation begins with a tightly specified grey-and-beige outfit. The assistant first
recommends dark-grey trousers for “contrast,” concedes that black creates more contrast, then
reverts to grey after Artiom paraphrases the recommendation. He notices the inconsistency but
still extracts one useful operational detail—matching socks can hide the ankle exposed when
skinny trousers ride up. The conversation then becomes a multilingual interrogation of the
model: Czech/Russian transliteration, Romanian etymology, Slavic language distance, Sanskrit,
French citizenship-test practice, AI regulation and post-work learning. Artiom repeatedly
presses for exact answers when the model hedges. The assistant confidently invents weak
percentages and a false-looking Latin derivation for Romanian *pricină*, illustrating that a
responsive correction loop is not a substitute for sourced verification. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14257-14374`.

**Product and coding probes.** On 12 October he iterates 29 times on a slogan for “PartyBox,”
a game app containing party games and a drinking feature called PartyShot. He rejects slogans
that make laughter the central promise and continues until the assistant can combine humour,
catchiness and the actual product structure. No retained message says that the app existed,
that a slogan was selected, or that he wrote the concept himself; it is evidence of product
copy direction, not a shipped product. Separate threads ask for a Python trivia game, then
reverse a GUI request into “with no GUI,” and ask how to build a beautiful mobile trivia game
from scratch in TypeScript. A number-pyramid exercise is run, rejected as not working, and
re-requested in a more compact form. These are early build-oriented probes with sparse outcome
evidence. Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:44-101`, `:178-199`, `:210-215`,
`:340-345`.

**Practical scarcity and account-security scenario.** On 28 October a long iPhone thread
describes a device left unused for months, unavailable local Apple support, insufficient money
for a service centre, forgotten login details and a foreign purchase. The first-person prompt
also says an iTunes password had been given to somebody and asks whether they could remotely
prevent booting. Artiom subsequently seeks a self-service recovery/factory-reset path and then
a tiny square, all-touchscreen MP3 player like the sixth-generation iPod nano. The present-day
operator correction—that he does not think he ultimately gave his details to anyone—prevents
the password sentence from being promoted as a settled historical disclosure. The practical
constraints and desire for a direct, affordable, self-service path remain attributable to the
conversation. The assistant supplies several inaccurate or obsolete device comparisons, so
its product recommendations are not reliable evidence. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:1-34`.

**Language learning and source transformation.** The month includes French tense drills and
a carefully iterated pen-pal introduction in which Artiom estimates his French no higher than
B2, catches a confused synonym answer, and asks for singular informal address. Ukrainian
diction exercises and rhymed translations contain large supplied literary/lyric passages;
those source texts must not be treated as his composition or autobiography. He challenges
translation choices for aggression, kinship and register and supplies his own alternatives,
which is evidence of comparative-language judgement. Evidence:
`chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:138-161`, `:1264-1321`, `:14498-14527`,
`:14643-14668`, `:15058-15061`.

**Immediate life and preference signals.** A one-turn message says he is meeting a woman from
his university the next day after first meeting online and is low on money; the assistant
suggests a walk or picnic, with no retained outcome. Another asks for a place where food can
remain on a table overnight without pests, reinforcing a concrete quality-of-life preference
but not a destination decision. Questions about second-largest cities, easiest languages,
European demographics, religion and a phrase about deserving worldly success are inquiries,
not admissions of belief or intent. Evidence: `chatgpt_conversations_gpt2_2026.jsonl:14412-14413`,
`:14972-14973`, `:14460-14465`, `:14569-14578`.

**Chronological consequence.** October adds a credible ancestor of the current assistant
goal: not a “virtual twin” yet, but an explicit interest in machine continuity, maintenance,
purpose and human–machine cognitive analogy. At the same time, the month strengthens the more
ordinary mechanism that later makes such a system useful—Artiom specifies a real constraint,
tests the answer, notices contradiction or vagueness, preserves the useful fragment and asks
again. It also reinforces the need for separate truth, provenance and outcome layers: much of
the assistant's confident language, product and consumer advice is wrong or unverified.

### November 2023 — coordinate systems, travel execution and foundational coding study

**Record quality:** high for interaction mechanics and several immediate plans, moderate for
biography. Fifty-one conversations contain 167 human and 160 assistant text turns. Six
near-identical “translate rat into twenty languages” conversations were opened within two
minutes without retained assistant text; they are retry/interface duplication, not six
independent interests.

**A designed system rather than a requested list.** The month's longest conversation starts
as RPG-class naming but becomes a magic-system design session. Artiom imposes one-word class
names, rejects specialisations already assigned to a rogue, and requires a broad antithesis
between priest and warlock. He then supplies the core model himself: magic is energy
manipulation represented on coordinates, with order/light versus chaos/dark on one axis and
energy/void versus matter on another. He corrects the model's metaphysical interpretation of
void, rejects proposed third dimensions for being non-polar, too specific or redundant with
order/chaos, pauses when the search degrades, and later asks it to position known fictional
schools on the coordinates. This is attributable systems/world design: the framework comes
