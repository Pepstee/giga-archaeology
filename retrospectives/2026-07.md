## Directional contract — proposed, not ratified

These are compensating guardrails for the ambition, not a request to make the ambition smaller.

1. **External-proof gate.** No new non-security internal programme capability until one external outcome is closed: a paid Northern Ray delivery, a launched customer-facing artefact with independent use, a job offer, or an equally auditable substitute. Choose the exact outcome and date.
2. **One scoreboard.** Weekly record only falsifiable career signals: customer conversations, proposals, paid work, launches, users, references, interviews, and public case studies. Track internal tests separately; never substitute them.
3. **Productise the proof.** Make Travel Designer a genuine P0 drill with a named user, real infrastructure, and visible outcome. An internal demo does not clear the gate.
4. **Keep the two-track life explicit.** Korean/Hyun/travel are not rewards after success; they are part of the life the career supports. Set commitments as deliberately as technical milestones.
5. **Separate wealth from cash flow.** Maintain a private evidence pack for the crypto and fiat trail, and make the career scoreboard measure recurring earned value rather than portfolio movements. Obtain professional tax advice before filing or restructuring transactions.
