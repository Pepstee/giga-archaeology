# Artiom, reconstructed across the full retained corpus

**Prepared:** 26 July 2026  
**Coverage:** two Discord accounts, Telegram, full two-sided Instagram, Claude and two
ChatGPT exports; 532,274 unified observations  
**Status:** interpretive synthesis awaiting operator correction  
**Evidence system:** `corpus/derived/unified_memory_20260726.sqlite3`

## The shortest accurate characterisation

Artiom is a **sovereignty-seeking builder who learned agency inside a social game-world, was
injured by interpersonal ambiguity, and gradually transferred the same drive for mastery into
products, performance, self-engineering and autonomous systems**.

He has an auditor's relation to truth, an aesthete's relation to coherence and a competitor's
relation to worth. He wants life to be both beautiful and legible: the mechanism should be
understood, the hierarchy should be real, the result should be difficult to dismiss and the
whole thing should feel authored rather than inherited.

The central strength and central defence are the same. When something threatens him, he
builds a model of it. The model often makes him unusually effective. It can also become a
fortress from which feelings, other people and ordinary uncertainty are treated as hostile
inputs rather than parts of life.

## The twelve strongest patterns

### 1. He totalises domains

Artiom rarely leaves an important interest as “something I do.” It becomes a world with
mechanics, ranks, artefacts, language and a route to sovereignty.

WoW was the first complete example: performance, community, identity and daily progression.
Sales became mentors, first impressions, scripts, leadership, a five-month note corpus and a
book. Appearance became wardrobe systems, facial geometry, skincare, procedures and SMV.
University became a 100%-targeting dissertation architecture. AI became agents, gates,
orchestration, memory and now a digital twin.

This is why he can progress rapidly: adjacent tasks reinforce one another. It is also why
loss of interest is existential rather than local. A totalised domain is carrying more than
its official purpose.

### 2. He seeks the governing mechanic

In November 2020 he did not answer a WoW stat question with a cap number. He separated direct
damage, auto-attacks, energy procs, poisons and competing marginal values. Later he uses the
same cognitive grammar:

- What actually causes the outcome?
- Which variable is confused with another?
- What is the second-order effect?
- What is the real bottleneck?
- Which proof would falsify the story?

This is a genuine analytical strength. The recurring error is assuming that human
relationships have a discoverable mechanic in the same way a designed game or software
system does. People can be inconsistent, plural and only partly known without one hidden law
that resolves them.

### 3. His self-worth is conditioned on exceptionality

The goal is not normally “be competent.” It is be the best character, outperform 95%, produce
a 100% dissertation, become the man other men fear competing with, or build the most serious
project of his life.

This is not just boasting. It is a method for defeating shame and lost time. If the result is
exceptional, then the period before it can be redeemed. The cost is that normal progress
cannot provide stable satisfaction. The evaluator moves the threshold after each success.

His healthiest competitive frame is artefact-relative: “Does this system work better? Did
this solve a real problem?” His least healthy frame is existence-relative: “If I am not
superior here, what is left to like about me?”

### 4. His real discipline is selective devotion

The corpus contains both extreme output and admitted half-completion. There is no
contradiction. Artiom is highly conscientious *after* a domain earns devotion and much less
dutiful before it does. He named the distinction himself: motivation is unreliable,
discipline alone is insufficient, and obsession must be channelled.

This creates bursts of remarkable construction and periods of aversion, abandonment or
identity replacement. The 2019 attempt to give away his WoW account, the later return, sales,
SCAFAD, wardrobe, dating analysis and orchestrator all fit the pattern.

The developmental requirement is not “find stronger obsession.” It is to build a small
maintenance floor that survives when symbolic charge falls, while reserving obsession for
work that has passed an external-value test.

### 5. He began socially central, then became selectively concentrated

The teen corpus is crucial here. In 2021 he sent 125,299 text messages across 250 threads and
explicitly compared knowing everyone across communities to school popularity. He monitored
whether friends withdrew and worried that inviting the wrong person would disturb a guild's
harmony.

The later claim that he is detached from almost everyone should therefore not be read as “he
cannot connect.” He demonstrably can navigate groups, perform publicly, teach, sell and
maintain intense dyads. What changed is the felt value and concentration of connection.

His current risk is not social incompetence but **maintenance asymmetry**: he values a small
number of friends, assumes adulthood makes distance normal, then reaches out too rarely for
the relationship to receive evidence of that value.

### 6. He uses humour as atmosphere, armour and dominance

The old Discord voice constantly moves between expertise and absurdity. Profanity can be
aggressive, but it is also affiliative punctuation. Emoji floods, mock-grand language,
sexualised copypasta and exaggerated masculinity create a shared theatre.

Humour serves three functions:

1. make a group feel like its own world;
2. avoid staying exposed after sincerity;
3. control the frame by making the other person react inside his performance.

The first is socially creative. The second protects him. The third can become cruel when the
other person is not consenting to be a prop.

### 7. Ambiguity is experienced as loss of control

On 8 May 2022, while asking Sasha what they would do, he clarified that he was not forcing
her and then named the problem: “Просто неопределенность какая-то.” In August he built a
model from her recent behaviour and explicitly left room for being mistaken. More than a
year later, Allen's disclosure mattered partly because it converted a long ambiguous injury
into a fact pattern.

This pattern later expands into adversarial truth-seeking, corpus construction and source-of-
truth architecture. It is productive when ambiguity can be reduced by evidence. It becomes
counterproductive when more inspection cannot supply the missing interior state of another
person.

He needs a stopping rule: after a reasonable evidence threshold, uncertainty is no longer a
research failure. It is a condition to accept, negotiate or leave.

### 8. He translates vulnerability upward into analysis

After arena conflict with Sasha, he analysed affect, expectations, recurrence and preventive
procedure. After romantic injuries, he moved into attachment theory, game theory,
Schopenhauer, blackpill, biology and behavioural experiments. In 2025 he asked for the
metacognition behind his metacognition.

The translation is intelligent: emotion becomes a tractable object. But a feeling described
perfectly is not necessarily felt, mourned or metabolised. Analysis can also redistribute
responsibility by selecting a model whose conclusion is emotionally useful.

His psychological development depends on keeping both representations:

- the causal model;
- the first-person sentence that does not solve anything, such as “I wanted her to choose
  me,” “I am ashamed,” or “I miss the world we had.”

### 9. He oscillates between severe self-blame and defensive control

The Sneg sequence shows both poles in one relationship. He can condemn himself globally,
apologise and place the other person above him. Then he can restore agency by controlling the
interpretation, withdrawing reciprocity or deciding which conclusion is permissible.

Later, the wish for a partner who follows him even when he is wrong is the controlling pole
stated openly. The December 2025 “nothing about me that I like” episode is the self-punitive
pole turned against the entire person.

The shared mechanism is over-accountability. If he is wholly at fault, he can change himself.
If his model is wholly right, he can control the frame. Mutual partial responsibility is
harder because it preserves uncertainty and dependence.

### 10. Loyalty is real but bounded and asymmetric

He values loyalty, reciprocity and alliance strongly. In practice, this appears as presence,
technical help, advice, protection, logistical planning and willingness to stand with chosen
people. Yet he has also said he wants loyalty independent of rightness, and he can discount
obligations to unknown people.

This means his moral structure is relational rather than universalist. “My person” has a
different status from “a person.” That can produce formidable friendship and partnership.
Its shadow is moral tunnel vision: harm becomes less salient when it occurs outside the
chosen circle or serves the alliance.

The needed refinement is not to abandon loyalty. It is to distinguish loyalty to a person's
long-term good and dignity from obedience to their immediate action.

### 11. Aesthetic coherence is a meaning channel

Clothes, grooming, cities, product interfaces, literature and controlled environments are
not side hobbies. They are evidence that a life has been deliberately composed. He resists
being reduced to shallow talking points about style or travel precisely because aesthetics
is meant to express a deeper integration, not substitute for one.

The danger is converting the aesthetic surface into a supposedly objective worth metric.
When beauty becomes a scoreboard, taste loses its capacity to give pleasure and becomes
another tribunal.

### 12. He compresses effort into leverage

The “god project” is an explicit example: one excellent orchestrator should demonstrate
automation design, software development and AI integration. The sales book preserves five
months of learning. The unified memory graph should answer biography, relationships,
fine-tuning and future retrieval.

This is one of his strongest entrepreneurial instincts. The counterweight is external proof.
A lever is valuable only if it is connected to something outside the private architecture:
users, customers, evaluators, friends, revenue, publication or lived experience.

## How his thinking changed

### 2018–2019: entry into a consequential world

The data begins sparsely. By late 2019 he is learning that competence creates access and that
usefulness creates belonging. The object of thought is bounded: raids, guilds, mechanics and
the people around them.

### 2020: mechanistic mastery becomes identity

He learns to reason in systems at high resolution. The game provides immediate feedback and
a social audience. Thought is concrete, comparative and embodied in daily practice. Private
friendships thicken around the shared world.

### 2021: social mechanics become community architecture

He no longer only masters the game. He moves across groups, values information access,
protects harmony and performs a recognisable persona. Status and belonging are fused: to be
known everywhere is to have doors open.

### 2022: attachment exposes the limit of system mastery

The Sneg bond moves attention from a legible game into another person's reciprocity. He tries
the same tools—performance, repair, inference, procedural change—but cannot force certainty.
The May crisis, June silence, July benediction and August ending create a template in which
intimacy is bound to ambiguity, shame and loss of epistemic control.

### 2023: the world outside the game enters the same frame

Work, university, body, documents, romantic conflict and software become integrated planning
objects. AI becomes a prolific junior collaborator. Relationship pain is increasingly
translated into general principles. The major change is not intelligence but scope: the
model now attempts to explain life, not only a game.

### 2024: competence becomes publicly performed

Tutoring and especially direct sales train rhetoric, first impressions, persuasion,
leadership and reading incentives. He writes the sales book to preserve and transmit
experience, then registers Excellence Exalted and employs a first teacher. The Sovereign
Architect first appears in practice: create a small institution whose rules and knowledge he
owns.

### 2025: the self becomes the principal engineering project

Metacognition, appearance, dissertation, habits and personae are integrated into one
self-cultivation system. SCAFAD converts ambition into technical evidence. But September
anhedonia and December global self-condemnation show that mastery cannot by itself answer
what life is for. Late-December doctrine converts relational vulnerability into moral and
biological abstraction.

### 2026: architecture becomes operational—and relational life continues

AI becomes governed labour, not only advice. The orchestrator, persistent machines, memory
protocols and corpus are attempts to make agency resumable and independent of one context
window or mood. At the same time, travel and Hyun provide embodied experience, care and
explicit happiness. This is not a clean choice between machine and life; it is the attempt to
make machinery secure the freedom to live.

The unresolved question is whether the infrastructure remains subordinate to that life.

## Lowest point

There are at least three different lows, and calling one “the lowest” without type would be
misleading.

### Most acutely dangerous documented low: 25 May 2022

In real time, amid an unsafe apartment, a fight, money conflict and overwhelm, Artiom said
actions he might take could lead to his death and begged Sasha not to call. This is the
strongest immediate-risk evidence in the retained corpus. It does not establish intent or a
diagnosis.

### Strongest global collapse of self-evaluation: 2–3 December 2025

He moved from a socially active day to “there is nothing about me that I like,” combining
money, teeth, driving, citizenship, home, friends, appearance and wasted youth into one
negative verdict. The breadth of condemnation makes it the strongest later self-worth
collapse.

### Strongest meaning/anhedonia low: September 2025

“I just want to not” is less acute than May 2022 but more existentially empty. Work and home
both become states defined by the wish not to occupy them.

## Happiest point

The strongest directly documented candidate remains 24–25 April 2026. The evidence combines
successful autonomous work, explicit intense happiness, playful intimacy, “Working is fun”
and “Life is good x2.” It synchronises agency, mastery, chosen attachment and embodied ease.

The old Discord account cannot displace that conclusion because volume does not prove
happiness. 2021 may have been the richest period of spontaneous belonging and shared world,
but it lacks an equally explicit private affect statement. It should be remembered as a
candidate for *aliveness and embeddedness*, not automatically the happiest year.

## What he wants

Under the changing objects, the wants are stable:

1. **Authorship:** a life whose direction and rules he chose.
2. **Sovereignty:** enough money, mobility, legal security and automation not to be trapped.
3. **Mastery:** real competence that survives comparison and adversarial testing.
4. **Proof:** external outcomes that make self-belief falsifiable.
5. **Aesthetic coherence:** body, clothes, home, city and work that feel deliberately composed.
6. **A consequential world:** projects and relationships that matter every day rather than
   generic routine.
7. **Chosen loyalty:** a few people with whom alliance is practical, durable and mutual.
8. **Preservation:** knowledge and memory that do not disappear when a job, account or
   conversation ends.
9. **Intensity without captivity:** he wants deep engagement but fears dependence on the
   person or institution that supplies it.

The deepest want may be phrased as: **to become powerful enough to choose his life without
making himself too defended to experience it.**

## How he should live

This is not a moral command; it is the life design most compatible with the evidence.

### Keep the architect, limit the fortress

Build systems for memory, work and execution. Do not use systems to eliminate every
unmeasurable human state. Some uncertainty must remain inside friendship, love, art and
identity.

### Maintain three simultaneous scoreboards

1. **External value:** shipped work, users, revenue, marks, reliability.
2. **Relational continuity:** who received time, care and honest contact this week.
3. **Lived experience:** what was enjoyed without needing to become leverage.

If only the first is tracked, sovereignty becomes isolation. If only the second is tracked,
he may feel captured. If only the third is tracked, ambition loses form.

### Separate worth from rank

Continue competing, but never let a local metric issue a global verdict on the person.
“This artefact is not yet excellent” is actionable. “There is nothing about me that I like”
is a category error.

### Use an ambiguity stopping rule

For important interpersonal uncertainty:

1. state the observation;
2. ask once directly;
3. distinguish what is known from inferred;
4. decide what boundary follows;
5. stop investigating unless new evidence arrives.

This preserves epistemic integrity without turning another person into an endless case file.

### Institutionalise maintenance

His life cannot rely only on obsession. A minimal weekly floor should survive every domain
cycle:

- one friend initiated;
- one piece of health/admin maintenance;
- one externally shipped unit;
- one unoptimised experience;
- one review of whether the current system still serves the global goal.

### Demand consent for domination and performance

Competitive banter, sexual intensity, dark humour and hierarchical play can all be valid
inside explicit mutuality. They become corrosive when “justice,” experimentation or leverage
is used to deny another person's equal subjectivity.

### Make the machine buy life

Every major infrastructure project should name the human freedom it is intended to create and
an observable date by which that freedom is exercised. If the orchestrator saves ten hours,
some of those hours should become travel, friendship, physical experience or public creation,
not only a larger orchestrator.

## Archetype

The best primary archetype remains **the Sovereign Architect**.

- *Sovereign* because autonomy, authorship and freedom from arbitrary dependence are the
  repeated higher-order goals.
- *Architect* because his native act is to identify structure, connect layers and build an
  environment in which action becomes possible.

The shadow archetype is **the Fortress Auditor**:

- everything is inspected but little is trusted;
- feelings become evidence objects;
- people become incentives, categories or failure modes;
- self-worth is permanently under hostile review;
- the system expands faster than the life it was meant to protect.

The developmental archetype is therefore not a different character. It is **the Hospitable
Architect**: still exacting, sovereign and formidable, but building worlds other people can
enter without surrendering their own agency—and allowing himself to inhabit them without
needing to control every room.

## Claims that remain weakest

- No corpus can determine a clinical diagnosis, IQ or fixed attachment style.
- “Sovereign Architect” is a useful compression, not an essence.
- The Sneg incoming side is absent; the moral sequence remains incomplete.
- The categorical beliefs about women are directly reported and behaviourally consequential,
  but their autobiographical origin does not establish their truth.
- 2021 may have been joyful or merely densely habitual; activity volume cannot decide.
- The present operator can correct event facts that no export contains. Such corrections
  should be appended as testimony, not used to rewrite the original evidence.
